# Theological Bridge — Master Equation Domain

## How the Theology Maps to the Mathematics

### The Impossibility (algebra)
```
J = T_A / D     (justice = fraction of damage restored)
M = 1 - T_B / D (mercy = fraction offender does not bear)
T_A = T_B        (closed system — budget must balance)
=> J = 1 - M     (justice + mercy = 1, always)
=> J + M + W = 3 is IMPOSSIBLE in any closed system
```

### The Resolution (theology + algebra)
```
T_ext = D        (external party absorbs full cost)
T_A = D          (victim fully restored — J = 1)
T_B = 0          (offender fully relieved — M = 1)
W_ext = 1        (cost-bearing is voluntary)
=> J + M + W = 3 (the impossible point, off the line)
```

### The Requirements for the Cost-Bearer
1. External to the ledger (not victim, not offender, not conscripted bystander)
2. Voluntary (forced substitution creates new injustice)
3. Full capacity to absorb without being destroyed
4. The cost must be REAL and fully absorbed (not partial, not symbolic)

### The Trinity Maps to the Mechanism

| Role | Algebraic Function | Trinitarian Person |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Authority to resolve | The Judge who can declare the debt settled | Father |
| Voluntary cost-bearer | T_ext = D, chosen freely, not destroyed | Son |
| Restoration agent | Applies healing without coercion (W = 1) | Spirit |

### The Altruism Lemma
Internal love (human forgiveness, sacrifice) moves you ALONG
the J + M = 1 line. It redistributes the cost. It does not
eliminate it. The victim who forgives absorbs the wound — noble,
but J decreases. The offender who makes restitution pays — just,
but M decreases.

Love INSIDE the system redistributes the cost.
Love OUTSIDE the system retires it.

That is not a slogan. It is what the algebra requires.

### What This Means for Every Domain
Every domain that deals with justice, mercy, or cost-bearing
(which is ALL of them) has a _theological/ bridge that traces
back here. The education system can't solve transmission failure
internally. The economic system can't resolve debt without
externalizing cost. The justice system can't rehabilitate
offenders without someone absorbing the deficit.

Every domain runs into J + M = 1.
Every domain needs the external cost-bearer.
Every _theological/ folder traces back to the Cross.
