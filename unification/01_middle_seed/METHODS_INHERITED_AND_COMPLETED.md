# Methods Inherited and Completed
## The Lineage Behind the UNIFICATION Method

UNIFICATION does not begin from nothing. It inherits real methods from earlier work, keeps what those methods did well, and adds controls where their programs became too broad, too loose, or too untestable.

The proper posture is neither “they failed and we alone succeeded” nor “we are merely repeating them.” It is this:

> They opened essential parts of the path. We inherit their methods, make the bridges explicit, and add the controls required for a cross-domain claim to survive serious review.

## 1. Whitehead: Coherence, Adequacy, and Applicability

### Method inherited

Whitehead's speculative method requires a general scheme to be consistent, coherent, adequate to experience, and applicable. His basic insight is that a comprehensive account cannot merely avoid contradiction; its core concepts must fit together and meet the world.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- Each primitive is a definition atom, not only prose in a book.
- Each application is a claim atom with sources, warrant labels, and kill conditions.
- Adequacy is not declared globally. It is decomposed into domain-level evidence and bridge records.
- Theology is explicitly identified as theology rather than being dissolved into a general metaphysics.

### Credit statement

Whitehead gave the project the demand for a coherent and adequate whole. UNIFICATION adds a source-traceable graph that shows where the whole actually reaches and where it does not.

## 2. General Systems Theory: Open Systems and Cross-Domain Form

### Method inherited

General systems theory searches for formal patterns of organization, openness, feedback, regulation, and hierarchy that recur across disciplines without requiring that every discipline use the same detailed equation.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- A socket is not an identity claim. Each native field retains its own equations and meanings.
- Every cross-domain correspondence has a stated grade and direction.
- A random-order negative control prevents a general pattern from becoming post hoc numerology.
- A bridge must name what it preserves and what it does not preserve.

### Credit statement

Systems theory showed that form can travel across domains. UNIFICATION adds the rule that form may travel only with a declared map, a control, and a failure condition.

## 3. Wheeler and von Weizsacker: Reconstruction from Informational Primitives

### Method inherited

Information-first programs attempt reconstruction: begin with a small operational grammar of distinctions or information, then recover physical structure rather than merely relabeling it.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- Every claimed reconstruction gets a recovery obligation: state which native result is recovered, under which assumptions, and with what limiting behavior.
- A slogan such as “information is fundamental” never counts as recovery.
- The information layer remains one strong socket among others until a derivation establishes more.

### Credit statement

Information-first physics taught the project to ask whether a deeper grammar can recover the world. UNIFICATION adds explicit recovery tests and refuses to count a verbal resemblance as derivation.

## 4. Intertheoretic Relations: Bridge Laws and Model Maps

### Method inherited

Philosophy of science treats relations between theories as requiring bridge principles or model mappings. A bridge is neither automatically a definition nor automatically an identity.

### What UNIFICATION adds

Every bridge record must declare:

```text
source domain and target domain
source quantity and target quantity
map or correspondence
assumptions and units
preserved structure
non-preserved structure
direction of inference
propagation behavior under falsification
rival map
failure condition
```

### Credit statement

Intertheoretic work taught the project that a bridge is a substantive object requiring its own defense. UNIFICATION makes that object first-class in the atom graph.

## 5. Noether and Thermodynamics: Universal Constraint Without Replacement

### Method inherited

Noether-style and thermodynamic reasoning show how a domain-general constraint can govern many systems without replacing their detailed local equations.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- “Constraint-class unification” is a project hypothesis, not an inherited scientific category that proves itself by association.
- A proposed universal constraint must state its symmetry, conservation relation, boundary condition, or invariant mathematically.
- The claim must recover known domain behavior and exclude a nearby alternative.

### Credit statement

These precedents show that a universal constraint can unify without reduction. UNIFICATION takes on the harder burden of showing that its own constraint grammar is genuinely nontrivial.

## 6. Barbour and Science-Theology Integration: Explicit Relation Types

### Method inherited

Science-and-religion scholarship distinguishes conflict, independence, dialogue, and integration rather than treating all cross-domain talk as one thing.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- A precise label system: D, C, E, P, H, T, A, and O.
- Christian theological identification is allowed and named; it is never silently converted into empirical proof.
- Historical claims about Christ are assigned historical evidence requirements rather than hidden inside a physics bridge.

### Credit statement

Integration scholarship established that science and theology can be related without one becoming the other. UNIFICATION adds a formal claim ledger for that relation.

## 7. Adversarial Collaboration: Theory-Neutral Tests and Rival Symmetry

### Method inherited

The COGITATE consciousness program placed competing theories under a theory-neutral, preregistered, adversarial testing design rather than allowing each theory to select only friendly evidence.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- The Rival Model Fairness Trial becomes mandatory, not optional.
- Theophysics, IIT, global-workspace approaches, QBism where relevant, process metaphysics, and generic theism receive the same socket questions, grades, and negative controls.
- The result can be mixed. A rival winning a socket is information, not sabotage.

### Credit statement

Adversarial collaboration supplies the project’s most important social method: a theory is stronger when its strongest opponents help define what would count against it.

## 8. Formal Verification and Software Testing: Executable Guardrails

### Method inherited

Formal proof verifies statements under explicit assumptions. Software testing uses invariants, negative controls, regression tests, and property tests to catch violations that prose misses.

### What UNIFICATION adds

- Lean proof labels never become theological or empirical labels by implication.
- Canon gates block retired notation and deprecated claims from re-entering new answers.
- The random-order trial functions as a negative control.
- API routing is governed by labels, status, and kill conditions rather than only prompt wording.

### Credit statement

Formal methods taught the project that an untyped claim can leak through a system. UNIFICATION applies that lesson to cross-domain reasoning itself.

## The Completed Method

The method UNIFICATION is trying to finish is:

```text
1. Define primitives before arguing from them.
2. Preserve native-domain authority.
3. Treat every bridge as a separately testable object.
4. Make assumptions, units, parameters, and uncertainty visible.
5. Require recovery of known results where a derivation is claimed.
6. Require a negative control and a failure condition.
7. Test rivals under identical rules.
8. Keep theological identification explicit and accountable to its own sources.
9. Route API answers through governance so the system cannot quietly overclaim.
```

That is the nod to the people who came before: they supplied the pieces. The project’s distinctive work is to make the pieces answerable to one another.
