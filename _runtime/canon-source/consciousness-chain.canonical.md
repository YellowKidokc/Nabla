Χ.2

### Mind of Christnous Christou (νοῦς Χριστοῦ)

No gradients. Uniform, undistorted awareness. No partiality, no local blindness, no perspective distortion.

Equilibrium Property

∂_μχ → 0 — all spatial and temporal gradients vanish The field is the same everywhere and everywhen. No distortion.

Physical Meaning

When all gradients vanish, there is no local variation. Every point in the field has the same value. Applied to consciousness: complete, impartial, undistorted awareness of all things simultaneously.

Scripture Pattern

"But we have the mind of Christ" (1 Cor 2:16). "For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face" (1 Cor 13:12). The zero-gradient state is the "face to face" — no distortion remaining.

Anti-term: Distortion / Partiality

∂_μχ ≠ 0. Local gradients. Some regions have more awareness, some less. Bias, blindness, selective perception. The human condition — we see through a glass darkly because our χ-field has gradients.

Status

STRONG. ∂_μχ → 0 is the definition of equilibrium for a scalar field. The theological mapping (gradients = distortion) is natural but needs formal validation that it's not just analogy.

Scripture Pattern

Superluminal propagation. Effects before causes. Acting on information that hasn't arrived yet — which is either prophecy (legitimate, from the source) or presumption (illegitimate, from the self). Folly is treating your guesses as if they were causal knowledge.

MEDIUM. Causal propagation is proven (Paper 3, Sec 5.1). The mapping to wisdom is suggestive but "respects order" is a broad concept. Does this overlap with Chain 6's "Counsel" (Bayesian optimal)?
