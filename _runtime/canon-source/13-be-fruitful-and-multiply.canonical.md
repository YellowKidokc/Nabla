Move 1 · Remove Math Overnight — and Accountability Dies

Suppose stable mathematical relations drift tonight. Not our knowledge of them — the relations themselves. Quantity drifts and theft becomes undetectable. Proportion drifts and negligence cannot be shown. Sequence drifts and promises become unstable. Identity drifts and evidence decouples from fact. Measurement drifts and medicine cannot dose.

Read the casualty list: **negligence · fraud · stewardship · justice · accountability**. These are moral categories, and every one presupposes that a claim can be compared against a fact.

remove stable mathematics → remove reliable comparison → remove trustworthy measurement → remove predictable consequence → undermine accountability → moral life becomes unintelligible

The twist that strengthens it: a world where math stops working may not be a possible world at all. Math is not furniture inside reality — it is part of what makes reality cohere. **Status: strong.** No hostile review has touched this move.

Move 1B · Remove the Laws — and Fruitfulness Dies DAVID'S FLANK

David's counterfactual is the clean one — not lawlessness, _unreliable_ law: **gravity works for thirty seconds, every ten to a hundred years, unpredictably.**

The casualty is not physics homework. It is fruitfulness. No stable strong force, no nuclei, no soil. No stable gravity, no plumb line, no orbit. Effort stops mapping to outcome — and then you cannot build, you cannot plant, **you cannot multiply**. Not arithmetic-multiply: multiply your acreage, your herd, your house, your line.

remove stable laws → remove reliable cause and effect → remove work → remove production, growth, multiplication → remove stewardship → remove the possibility of a farm, a family, a civilization

And the mandate layer: "Be fruitful and multiply" is a task that presupposes a stable platform. You cannot steward what drifts. The stability of the laws is not merely a constraint on creation — **it is a provision for it**. The structure is given _for_ us, not against us. Moves 2–3 · Why the Pressure Moves Upward — and May Not Stop

**Science cannot ground its own tools.** Every experiment already presupposes identity, noncontradiction, valid inference, quantity, relation, measurement. Proving those experimentally would be circular. Formal backbone — Chaitin: `Ground(Math) ∉ Math`. No system justifies the full complexity of the truths it uses.

**Humans cannot be the ground.** Human systems drift — laws, languages, currencies, institutions, models. A valid mathematical relation does not drift when people become dishonest, tired, political, or confused. We are fallible _participants_ , not the source. And math predates every mind by 13.8 billion years.

If human beings did not make mathematical truth, and science cannot ground it without circularity, then refusing an upward ground requires an explanation for why the chain is allowed to stop at an unexplained abstraction.

"Brute fact," "abstract objects simply exist," "logic is just a feature of thought" — stopping points, not explanations. **Status: strong.**

Move 5 · The Keystone — the Ground Cannot Be Deceptive

`A8` truth requires grounding · `A9` ground ≠ nothing (K(∅)=0 → Output(∅)=∅) · `A10` ground ≠ chaos (K(Tₘ) ≪ |Tₘ| → ¬Random) · `A11` **ground ≠ deceptive** (¬Deceptive(Tₘ) → ¬Deceptive(Ground)).

Deception is divergence between appearance and reality. Mathematical truth has none: 2+2 appears to equal 4, and does. A deceptive source cannot reliably produce non-deceptive output. The output is non-deceptive. **Therefore the source is non-deceptive.**

Non-deceptiveness is a moral property. Truthfulness is a virtue in every moral system that has ever existed. Even the liar must pretend to be truthful. A moral property — derived from information theory, not imported from theology.
