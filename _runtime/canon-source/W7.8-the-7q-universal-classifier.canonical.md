# The 7Q Universal Classifier

Fourteen worldviews, one test, run all the way through. Every hostile question left in, on purpose.

Q0Existence

Why is there something rather than nothing?

CONTINUES — 14 SURVIVE

Something exists. Existence is the first bit — $1 \neq 0$. All fourteen worldviews on the table survive this one.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Show absolute nothingness is coherent and stable. The Buddhist śūnyatā reading is the only genuine challenge on record.

Q1Distinction

Can anything be distinguished?

CONTINUES — 13 SURVIVE*

Distinction is the first operation. For anything to be knowable, it must be distinguishable from something else. All substrates pass.

*The source data lists 13 survivors here against 14 at Q0 with no elimination logged — a counting inconsistency worth fixing before this goes public.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Show existence without distinguishability. Advaita Vedanta's non-dual challenge is real and needs a full steelman, not a dismissal.

Q2The Substrate Test

What is fundamental — matter, mind, information, math, relations, or brute fact?

Six candidates, tested one at a time against the same standard: does it explain itself without circularity or collapse?

Q2-A · MATTER — DIES
Rejected. Matter requires information to define itself; particles are, structurally, equation solutions. Structural realism (Ladyman/French) is the strongest counter on record.

Q2-B · MIND — DIES
Rejected on the same grounds: mind requires informational distinctions to operate at all. Idealism (Kastrup) claims mind is primary; the counter is that mind still implies information processing underneath.

Q2-C · INFORMATION — CONTINUES
Distinguishability IS information — a definitional identity, not an inference. No circularity, no regress. This is the node the rest of the chain is built on.

Q2-D · MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE — PARTIAL
Collapses into Q2-C if patterns just are information — or dies later at Q5 if math has no semantic content without a reader. Tegmark's Mathematical Universe Hypothesis is the live version.

Q2-E · RELATIONS — PARTIAL
Collapses into Q2-C (relations are information about the things related) or dies at Q3 for lack of anything to relate. Ontic structural realism is the serious version.

Q2-F · BRUTE FACT — DIES
Not wrong. Just done. Russell's "the universe is just there" is honest and legitimate — it costs all explanatory power, and that's the whole cost.

7 SURVIVE

Idealism, Dualism, Panpsychism, Process, Classical Theism, Panentheism, Christianity. Pure materialism eliminated on structural grounds — not theological ones.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Show a physical property that isn't derivable from yes/no questions. Structural realism is still the strongest counter.

Q3Order / Structure

Must information be organized to be meaningful?

CONTINUES — 7 SURVIVE

Random bits are noise. Coherence is necessary, and it admits degrees. Micro-coherence is conserved; macro-coherence cannot self-increase — that split keeps this consistent with the Second Law rather than fighting it.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Show meaningful information without organization — a genuinely random string that carries meaning. Kolmogorov complexity is uncomputable in general.

Q4Grounding the Ground

What grounds the ground itself?

Q4-A · BRUTE FACT TERMINUS — HONEST STOP
No regress, no self-grounding — just acceptance. Secular physics stops here, and stopping here is legitimate. Deism stops at this exact node.

Q4-B · INFINITE REGRESS — DIES
Gödel, Tarski, and Russell together: self-reference has limits, and an infinite regress never actually reaches ground. Process philosophy (Whitehead) argues reality is process without ground — that counter needs engagement.

Q4-C · SELF-GROUNDING — CONTINUES
$\chi = \int(\mathcal{G} \cdot K)\,d\Omega$ — a self-instantiating field, information as its own ground.

This is a framework commitment, not a proof. Q4-A (the honest stop) is a legitimate, un-refuted alternative — the choice of Q4-C over Q4-A is a choice, and it's named as one here rather than smuggled in as the only option.

6 SURVIVE

Idealism, Panpsychism, Process, Classical Theism, Panentheism, Christianity.

Q5Properties of the Ground

What properties must the ground have?

CONTINUES — 5 SURVIVE

Derived: self-consistent, maximally coherent, generative, non-temporal, non-material. Observer capacity ($\Phi$) defined here. Panpsychism starts dying — it has no measurement boundary once $\Phi$ is formally defined.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** The jump from syntax to semantics — from "$\Phi$ exceeds a threshold" to "there is something it is like to be this system" — has no published bridge anywhere. Searle's Chinese Room sits directly on this gap. This blank stays blank, on purpose.

Q6Observer Requirement

Does observation require a participant?

CONTINUES — 4 SURVIVE

Measurement requires participation. Copenhagen formalized; Bell nonlocality confirmed. Atheism dies here: no terminal observer means the measurement problem has no resolution under this framework's terms.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Show measurement without observer participation — Many-Worlds resolving everything without collapse. Many-Worlds is not definitively excluded here. QBism is also live. This is an acknowledged interpretive choice, not a forced conclusion.

Q7Terminal Observer

Does the observer chain terminate?

CONTINUES — 4 SURVIVE

The von Neumann chain must terminate somewhere — something with $\Phi = \infty$ has to exist, or the chain runs forever. Wigner said consciousness terminates it; Penrose said gravity does; von Neumann said it must terminate but never said what does it.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** $\Phi = \infty$ for a terminal observer is an assertion, not a proven result — there's no mathematical demonstration that infinite integrated information is even coherent. Renormalization-style handling may be required.

Q8The Eight Boundary Conditions

What must be true of the terminal observer?

Eight conditions, tested individually, each a candidate requirement for whatever sits at the end of the chain.

BC1 — TERMINAL OBSERVER EXISTS

Required directly by Q7's own termination logic. Without it: no measurement, no physics, no science.

BC2 — GRACE EXTERNAL TO SYSTEM

Negentropy targeting moral entropy specifically — not ordinary thermodynamic negentropy. Without it, the Second Law wins outright.

This is the exact seam where physics and theology actually separate — named, not hidden.

BC3 — MEASUREMENT ORTHOGONALITY

$[\hat{O}, \hat{\Phi}] = 0$ — basis-restricted knowledge, not unlimited omniscience. Perfect knowledge of moral orientation while respecting freedom in incompatible observables.

BC4 — THREE OBSERVERS REQUIRED

$N = 2$ under-determines (no closure); $N \ge 4$ claimed to reduce to an $N = 3$ basis via graph theory.

The N=3 minimality claim has no worked proof — deferred to an appendix not in this dataset.

BC5 — SUPERPOSITION PRESERVED

Without genuine superposition, moral responsibility becomes illusion — agents need to actually be undecided until they choose. Compatibilism (Frankfurt-style) is a live counter.

BC6 — INFINITE ENERGY SOURCE

A finite source eventually exhausts — heat death guaranteed without an infinite one. Deism confirmed dead here: a non-interacting deist god can't function as ongoing source.

BC7 — INFORMATION CONSERVATION

Lost information means destroyed identity — no continuity without conservation.

The black hole firewall paradox shows real, unresolved tension — in every framework, not just this one.

BC8 — VOLUNTARY COUPLING

Forced coupling collapses into determinism, making genuine choice impossible.

Most original claim, most exposed to challenge — no published physics theory addresses voluntary coupling directly.

3–4 SURVIVE

Process, Classical Theism, Panentheism, Christianity — with Deism confirmed dead at BC6.

Q9Identification

Do the derived properties match any existing claim?

CONTINUES — 3 SURVIVE

Yes — a claim made roughly two thousand years ago. Terminal observer plus all eight boundary conditions matches the Christian description of God. Atheism and Panpsychism formally confirmed dead.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Personhood is inserted here as its own separate axiom, not derived from the structural match — because structural match alone doesn't make something a person. A mathematical object could satisfy every boundary condition without being anyone. Flagged as irreducibly theological.

Q10Sign Structure (Moral Ontology)

Does moral distinction have ontological weight?

CONTINUES — 3 SURVIVE

Binary sign operator $\sigma = \pm 1$. Self-flip proven impossible. Salvation-by-works proven impossible. The Grace operator gets formalized here.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** The binary claim rests on choosing an $SU(2)$ symmetry group for morality — that's a modeling choice, not a forced deduction. A continuous moral state space with a different topology might not reduce to binary at all.

Q11Destiny

What are the long-term outcomes?

CONTINUES — 3 SURVIVE

Bimodal: an integration attractor (high-$\Phi$, "heaven") and a fragmentation attractor (low-$\Phi$, "hell"). Eschatology treated as physics.

**HOSTILE QUESTION** Show a single attractor instead of two — universal salvation. Universalism (Origen, von Balthasar) claims exactly that. If a coherent single-attractor model exists, this node needs real revision.

Q12The Uniqueness Test

Which tradition satisfies all eight boundary conditions?

FINAL NODE — 1 SURVIVES

Christianity: 8 of 8. Islam fails BC4 (no Trinity). Judaism fails BC-completion (no incarnation). Hinduism fails BC-uniqueness. Buddhism fails BC1 (no terminal observer).

**HOSTILE QUESTION — THE REAL ONE** Find another tradition scoring 8 of 8. Show the eight boundary conditions aren't exhaustive — does a BC9 exist? Show Christianity actually fails one of the eight. This node is explicitly named as the most vulnerable point in the entire chain to the charge of motivated reasoning — because the comparison assumes both exhaustiveness and cultural fairness, and neither is proven, only asserted.

## What This Chain Actually Is, and Isn't

This is an elimination structure, not a proof by construction. Every node that survives does so because the alternatives at that node fail a stated test — but several of those tests are the framework's own modeling choices (the $SU(2)$ symmetry at Q10, the $N = 3$ minimality at BC4, the choice of Q4-C over the equally-legitimate Q4-A), not forced deductions from prior steps.

The chain is honest about this throughout — every hostile question is left in, not answered away — which is what makes it worth taking seriously rather than what makes it complete.
