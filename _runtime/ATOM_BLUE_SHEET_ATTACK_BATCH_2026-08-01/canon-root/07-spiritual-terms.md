The salvation sequence (corrected canonical architecture)

Grace → voluntary coupling O(t) → salvation q: −1→+1 → justification → sanctification c(t) → glorification c→1

1

**Grace channel 𝒢** — idempotent, one-way, trace-preserving, fixes the positive state. Five impossibility theorems (Gödel, Tarski, Turing, Clausius, Landauer) force its five properties. Nine candidate classes eliminated; one survivor. G(t) = G₀·e^(∫r dt')·(1−R(t))

2

**Voluntary coupling O(t)** — gates the channel. The source is unconditional (G₀ > 0 always); reception is gated by consent. Grace is not limited by supply — it is limited by reception.

3

**Salvation q: −1→+1** — binary, non-reverting. The conversion jump L = |+⟩⟨−| is anticommuting and nilpotent (not idempotent — doesn't need to be). No reverse jump |−⟩⟨+| exists in the canonical operator set. Phase transition, ΔG < 0.

4

**Justification** — punctiliar status declaration: q = +1 locked. Not computed from internal state; declared by external authority. The absorbing positive sector formalizes non-reversion. Status(agent) := "righteous"

5

**Sanctification c(t)** — continuous coherence dynamics. Can rise and fall. Channel healing: D↓, A↑. The Master Equation governs this phase. ċ = −σc + β·O·Γ_ext·(1−c)

6

**Glorification c→1** — Lindblad attractor. V(ρ) → 0 monotonically. The channel no longer throttles the source. Sanctification completed.

**Key correction (July 2026):** A nonzero two-sector operator cannot be both strictly anticommuting with σ̂ and idempotent. The old single-operator formalism carried incompatible properties. The canonical repair distinguishes the conversion jump L (anticommuting, nilpotent) from the grace channel 𝒢 (idempotent, absorbing). Salvation (q) is binary and non-reverting. Sanctification (c) is continuous and can fluctuate. Two variables, two operators, no contradiction.

**Hybrid dynamics:** salvation and justification are fast, discrete events on the sign/status layer. Sanctification and glorification are slow, continuous coherence dynamics. In v3 notation: dX = Π_S(W∇χ dt + η_c dt + J dN_t).
