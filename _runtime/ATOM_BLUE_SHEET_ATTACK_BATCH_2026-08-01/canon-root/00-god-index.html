God Pages

A canon reading order for the God/Theophysics blue pages. The file names stay untouched for Cloudflare stability; the numbers here are shelf labels, not path names.

Reading rule: start with God and story, move through Trinity and Truth, then let the axioms carry the weight. The 7Q Classifier comes after the foundation because it is not the doorway; it is the gauntlet.
