A/B/C Topic

Spiritual Terms Dictionary

A reference shelf for recurring theological and bridge vocabulary. Terms keep their doctrine; physics does not replace them.

A - Theology

Terms Keep Their Doctrine

Grace, sin, faith, mercy, atonement, and redemption are not emptied into generic physics words. They are defined by Scripture, doctrine, and the salvation sequence: grace → voluntary coupling → conversion → justification → sanctification → glorification.

B - Science

O3B - Register Gap

Science does not define spiritual terms as doctrine. It can sometimes supply analogies or structural witnesses, but the doctrinal meaning belongs to theology. This is a deliberate gap, not a failure.

C - Theophysics

Vocabulary Guardrail

The dictionary prevents semantic drift. It helps mark when a term is being used theologically, physically, mathematically, or as bridge language.

Key locked terms: grace, sin, agency, freedom, truth, Logos, knowledge, love, judgment, grace-frame, moral conservation, coherence/Christ.

Key partial terms: faith (quantum-to-faith reduction still needs witness work), deception (Maxwell-gate discriminability work queued).

Correction discipline: in the v3 dictionary, S = Strong/Love, not entropy/sin. T = Thermodynamics/Judgment. R = Relativity/Grace-Frame. These rulings prevent the stale drift that killed earlier versions.
