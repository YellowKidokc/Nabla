A/B/C Topic

Opening Axioms

Existence, distinction, relation, and orientation are the minimal inquiry floor. The bridge question is whether they remain independent primitives or project from a deeper ground.

A - Theology

God First In Reality

Theology does not wait for physics to grant God permission to exist. It confesses God as the living ground of being, truth, relation, and goodness. In this register, the four axioms are not ultimate; they are created-order reflections of the one God read four ways: Father (existence), Son / Word (distinction), Spirit (relation), and the Way/Truth (orientation).

This is not a proof smuggled into the premises. It is the theological starting point, stated openly. The framework then asks whether the created order shows structural rhymes with that starting point.

B - Science

Inquiry Floor

Science needs something to model, distinguishable states, lawful relation between states, measurement, error correction, stable records, and public revisability. That gives the practical floor:

  • A1 — Existence: there is something to model.
  • A2 — Distinction: states can be differentiated.
  • A3 — Relation / Lawfulness: states interact in repeatable ways.
  • A4 — Orientation: some answers are better than others; truth beats error.

Physics can honestly say these are required for intelligible theory. Physics cannot honestly say, from these alone, that Christianity is true.

C - Theophysics

Bridge Map

Theophysics reads the four axioms as a relational grammar. Information follows from distinction in relation; order follows from relation plus orientation. The move from "relation" to "relational ontology" is a valid bridge candidate, not a settled physics derivation.

Lean status: P-00 has a gold ticket for guardrail support — distinction implies existence, and truth ranks above error under the defined orientation relation. Lean does not prove God, Christianity, χ, the Logos field, the Master Equation, moral realism, or a self-grounding substrate.

Moral-first candidate: the M-series proposes that the Good is primitive and physics is a projection of moral/coherence structure. This is preserved as candidate architecture, not promoted to canon proof.
