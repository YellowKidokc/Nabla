A/B/C Topic

Lean Verification

Lean is the gold-ticket receipt layer: it checks formal claims under definitions. It does not prove God, doctrine, or bridge meaning.

A - Theology

Formal Honesty Serves Truth

If God is truth, formal claims should be checkable where possible. But theology is not reduced to Lean syntax. Doctrine, interpretation, pastoral recognition, and bridge hypothesis remain distinct claim types.

B - Science / Mathematics

What Lean Checks

Lean can verify definitions, implications, uniqueness claims, and consistency inside a formal system. It cannot make an undefined bridge true, and it cannot convert assumptions into empirical facts. A gold ticket requires four things: a .lean file, a compiled build, a report, and an explicit statement of what Lean did not prove.

C - Theophysics

Gold Ticket Standard

The canon should show what Lean checked, where the file lives, and what Lean did not prove. That protects the reader from treating every formal-looking claim as a theorem.

Current gold tickets:

  • P-00 Opening Postulates — LEAN_GUARDRAIL_SUPPORTED. Checked: minimal inquiry definitions are internally coherent; distinction implies existence; truth ranks above error. Did not prove: God, Christianity, χ, Logos field, Master Equation, moral realism.
  • T-01 Hard Questions Posture — LEAN_GUARDRAIL_SUPPORTED. Checked: under defined posture predicates, the Job posture uniquely preserves honest horror, relationship, refusal to take God's seat, God's goodness, and conscience. Did not prove: historical interpretation of any passage, complete answers to hard texts, doctrine of Scripture.

Consciousness Lean target: formalize the L1–L4 dependency graph and prove conditional theorems such as no consciousness without representation, self-reference, and orientation. That would be a conditional theorem, not an empirical proof that humans are conscious.
