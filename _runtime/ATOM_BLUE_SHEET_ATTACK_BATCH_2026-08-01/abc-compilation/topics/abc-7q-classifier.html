A/B/C Topic

7Q Classifier

The 7Q gauntlet compares worldviews by consistency, explanatory cost, and what each framework must borrow or leave unexplained. It is a pressure test, not a final proof.

A - Theology

Commitments Tested

Theology enters with actual commitments: God, creation, sin, grace, and redemption. The classifier should not make those sound neutral or optional inside the theology register. The Christian claim is that a terminal observer plus eight boundary conditions matches the God described in Scripture.

B - Science

Not Empirical Physics

The classifier uses logic, consistency, and explanatory comparison. It is not a physics experiment, and worldview scoring should not be presented as laboratory measurement. Several nodes depend on interpretive choices in quantum foundations (Copenhagen, observer participation), with Many-Worlds and QBism as live alternatives.

C - Theophysics

Gauntlet, Not Final Proof

7Q is a pressure test. It asks what a worldview can explain without hidden subsidies. It helps expose gaps, but it does not replace the deeper argument.

Funnel snapshot:

  • Q0 — Existence: 14 worldviews survive.
  • Q1 — Distinction: 13 survive (Advaita exits).
  • Q2 — Substrate: 7 survive (materialism/naturalism exit on structural grounds).
  • Q4 — Self-grounding: 6 survive (brute-fact terminus is an honest stop).
  • Q6 — Observer required: 4 survive.
  • Q9 — Properties match existing claim: 3 remain (Classical Theism, Panentheism, Christianity).
  • Q10–Q12 — Boundary conditions: Christianity is the framework's claimed survivor.

Honesty notes: the N=3 minimality claim is asserted, not yet proven. The jump from syntax to semantics (Φ threshold to "something it is like") has no published bridge. Personhood is inserted as a theological axiom, not derived from structural match.
