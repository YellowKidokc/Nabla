A/B/C Topic

Information and Witness

Distinguished content in relation becomes information; meaning requires a witness who can receive, refuse, and respond.

A - Theology

Logos And Witness

Theology names the world as intelligible because it is created through Logos. A witness is not merely a mirror; it is a person capable of receiving truth and responding to it. "In the beginning was the Word" is a substrate claim: information is fundamental, and its source is personal.

B - Science

Distinction, Channel, Record

Information theory begins with distinctions and signals. Communication requires source, channel, and receiver. Physical information is constrained by noise, record stability, and error correction. Shannon's channel capacity, C = B log₂(1 + S/N), gives the limits of reliable transmission.

Landauer's principle adds that irreversible information operations have thermodynamic cost. Information is not free once it is physically enacted.

C - Theophysics

K And The Shannon Base Layer

K names meaningful information density in the grammar. The witness-field language marks the transition from unused potential to actualized record. The base layer is:

Λ_i = A_i log₂(1 + T_i / D_i)

where A is bandwidth, T is true signal / coherence, and D is drift / noise / sin. The no-drift limit is the theological denominator insight: fullness is the reference capacity against which the actual channel is measured.

Guardrails: K is not matter by itself. Phi is not yet a completed formal operator. Information theory alone does not prove God. Distinguishability is the floor of information; semantic meaning requires additional structure.
