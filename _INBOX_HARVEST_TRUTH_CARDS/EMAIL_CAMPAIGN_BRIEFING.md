## Campaign Architecture

### Per Institution:
1. **Initial email** — personalized to recipient's domain and published work
2. **Follow-up #1** (2 weeks) — "Following up on my previous email..."
3. **Follow-up #2** (4 weeks) — "I wanted to make sure this reached you..."
4. **Subsequent follow-ups** — on a cadence, documenting each send
5. **Track everything** — date, recipient, subject, response/no-response

### Documentation Strategy:
- Every email sent gets logged (date, institution, department, recipient, subject)
- No-responses are documented
- The log becomes evidence: "We contacted [institution] on [dates]. No response was received."
- This is the "I emailed you 40 times" ammunition

## Key Mistakes to Avoid

From the AI operations log and this session:
1. **Desktop Commander can't read files from G:\WEBSITES** — returns empty. Use PowerShell `Get-Content -Raw` instead
2. **PowerShell npx wrapper fails** — write .bat files and launch via cmd
3. **Don't use `cd /d` in Desktop Commander** — use `D: && cd D:\path` pattern
4. **WebFetch strips HTML** — converts to markdown, losing hero banners and structural elements. Verify locally
5. **Don't under-extract from large pastes** — when David gives a big block of text, extract EVERYTHING, not just the first few matches
