## THE BOUNDARY CONDITIONS

For a complete theory of ultimate reality, eight boundary conditions must be satisfied:

| BC | Statement | What It Requires |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| BC1 | Self-grounding | Ground cannot require external ground |
| BC2 | Information conservation | Information preserved through all transformations |
| BC3 | Measurement orthogonality | Distinct observations yield distinct information |
| BC4 | Three observers | N = 3 for complete self-reference |
| BC5 | Superposition preserved | Potentiality exists until collapse |

## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Islam

**CHARGE:** Islam claims absolute divine simplicity (Tawhid)—Allah has no internal distinctions.

**BC4 FAILURE:**

| Requirement | Islam's Position | Problem |
|-------------|------------------|---------|
| Self-knowledge | Allah knows Himself | But self-knowledge requires knower and known (T.1) |
| Self-love | Allah is al-Wadud (Loving) | But eternal love requires eternal beloved (T.2) |
| Internal plurality | Denied (shirk) | But BC4 requires N = 3 |

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Did Allah love before creation?" | "Yes, Allah is eternally loving..." | Whom did He love? Himself? That requires relation. Creation? That's not eternal. |
| "Does Allah know Himself?" | "Of course, He is all-knowing..." | Self-knowledge requires distinction between knower and known. How does absolute simplicity know itself? |
| "Can Allah be fully relational?" | "Allah relates to creation..." | Creation is contingent. Was Allah relational before creation? If not, He changed when He created. |

**MATHEMATICAL VERDICT:**
```
Islam: N = 1 (absolute simplicity)
BC4 requires: N = 3
1 ≠ 3
∴ Islam fails BC4
```

**Additional failures:**
- **BC7:** No clear concept of Terminal Observer with Φ = ∞
- **BC8:** Divine decree (qadr) tensions with voluntary coupling

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of mathematical incoherence.

| Trimurti (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva) | Three gods, not three Persons of one God | This is tritheism, not trinity (different essences) |
| Atman = Brahman | Individual self is identical to ultimate | Distinction collapses; no relation, no love |

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |

| "Is love ultimate?" | "Love is maya (illusion) at the highest level..." | Then love is not grounded in ultimate reality. But love IS (1 John 4:8). |

**MATHEMATICAL VERDICT:**
```
Hinduism:
- If impersonal Brahman: N = 0 (no persons)

BC4 requires: N = 3 AND |Essence| = 1
∴ Hinduism fails BC4 in all variants
```

**Additional failures:**

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of fundamental structural mismatch.
