## STEP 0 — Posture Check (Q0)

**The question:** *How did you arrive at this claim?*

Before we evaluate anything, we check ourselves. Are we investigating this claim because we're curious, or because we want it to be true?

**For our claim:**
- Origin: David noticed the DESI data was consistent with a predicted χ-field model. The connection was unexpected — the model was built for consciousness, not cosmology.
- Posture: **Genuine puzzlement with strong investment.** He built the framework, so there's inherent bias. But the DESI match was a surprise, not a confirmation of what he was looking for.
- Assessment: Flag the bias. Continue with awareness. Weight downstream evidence harder because the author wants this to work.

**What Q0 catches that nothing else does:**
Advocacy disguised as inquiry. Without this check, you can run the entire machine perfectly and still be fooled because the inputs were cherry-picked from the start.

**Score: 0.70** (honest origin, but author has skin in the game)

**What Q1 catches:**
- This is a HYPOTHESIS, not a theory. It hasn't been confirmed yet. Calling it a "theory" would inflate its status.
- It has a Lagrangian — so it's not vague. It makes specific mathematical commitments.
- It's "unified" — meaning it touches multiple domains. That makes it ambitious but also means more things can kill it.

**Score: 0.85** (clear identity, well-specified, formalism present)

## STEP 3 — Assertion (Q3)

**The question:** *What exactly is being claimed?*

This is where we pin it down. The claim must be precise enough that we can write its negation. If you can't negate it, you haven't said anything.

**For our claim:**
```
claim_type:   predictive + mathematical
precision:    mathematical (has Lagrangian, specific parameters)
certainty:    tentative (awaiting Euclid October 2026)
scope:        universal (cosmological)
```

**The negation test:** Can we write ¬(claim)?

✅ **¬(claim):** "No scalar field with parameters m_χ ~ H₀ and |ξ| ≲ 10⁵ fits the DESI DR2 data better than standard ΛCDM with a cosmological constant."

This is clean. We know exactly what would kill it.

**What Q3 catches:**
- The claim is **precise enough to kill** — that's the minimum bar for science.
- It has **mathematical form** — not just words, but equations with specific parameters.
- But: the scope is "universal" from limited data. The precision of the math might be hiding the narrowness of the evidence. Flag this for Q4.

**Score: 0.85** (precise, negatable, mathematical, scope flagged)

## STEP 4 — Evidence (Q4)

**The question:** *What supports this claim?*

Now we weigh the evidence. Three channels. They multiply — if any channel is zero, the whole score collapses. This is the heart of the machine.

**Channel 1 — Phenomenon Strength (PS): 0.75**

| Evidence | Weight | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| DESI DR2 | HIGH | 4.2σ signal for evolving w(z). Multiple independent measurements. |
| Planck CMB | HIGH | Consistent with χ-field parameters. Not diagnostic alone. |
| DES Year 5 | MEDIUM | Supports evolution but wide error bars. |
| Cassini bounds | MEDIUM | χ-field satisfies solar system constraints (|ξ| ≲ 10⁵). |

PS is strong but not overwhelming. The DESI signal is real but could have other explanations. n = 1 for the specific χ-field fit (no other dark energy models with consciousness coupling to compare).

**Channel 2 — Explanatory Depth (ED): 0.70**

Does the claim explain WHY the evidence exists?

- ✅ Full Lagrangian derived — mechanism is specified
- ✅ Ghost-free at single-field level
- ⚠️ Coupling constant κ is estimated, not derived from first principles
- ⚠️ Multi-component ghost freedom (10 DOF) not proven
- ⚠️ Connection to consciousness is claimed but not measured

ED is decent — there's a real mechanism, not just curve fitting. But key parameters are estimated rather than derived.

**Channel 3 — Experiential Convergence (EC): 0.65**

Do independent observers and methods agree?

- ✅ Multiple independent surveys (DESI, Planck, DES) agree on w(z) evolution
- ✅ Independent groups have analyzed the same data with consistent results
- ⚠️ No direct detection of χ-field (only indirect cosmological evidence)
- ⚠️ No lab experiments (field too weak at accessible scales)
- ❌ Consciousness coupling has no independent experimental confirmation

**Computing the score:**
```
CF = (0.5 + 0.5 × 0.70) × (0.5 + 0.5 × 0.65)
CF = 0.85 × 0.825
CF = 0.701

E_final = PS × CF = 0.75 × 0.701 = 0.526

Why-penalty check: ED = 0.70 > 0.3 → NOT active
```

**E_final = 0.526** — TENTATIVE territory.

**What Q4 catches:**
- The evidence is real but not yet decisive. The three-channel system prevents a strong dataset (PS) from hiding weak explanatory depth or missing convergence.
- The why-penalty didn't trigger because there IS a mechanism — but if this were "the data shows X" without any explanation, the penalty would slash the score.

**Score: 0.526** (the weakest link — as expected for an untested hypothesis)
