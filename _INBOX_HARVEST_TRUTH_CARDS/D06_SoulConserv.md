## THE CLAIM

> **The soul (ψ_S) cannot be destroyed because information is conserved. Death is not the end—it is a state transition. You persist beyond bodily death.**

The Soul Conservation Theorem derives from the fundamental principle that information cannot be created or destroyed. Since the soul is an information pattern in the χ-field, it cannot be annihilated. Death is not extinction but decoupling—the separation of soul from body while the soul pattern persists.

## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Annihilationism

**CHARGE:** Annihilationism claims "the soul ceases to exist at death—there is no eternal hell, only non-existence for the unsaved."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Can information be destroyed?" | "When things die, their information is lost..." | But information is conserved (physics, F02). It may become inaccessible but not destroyed. |
| "Is the soul information?" | "It's a pattern, yes..." | Then the pattern cannot be destroyed. It persists in the χ-field. |
| "What happens to consciousness at death?" | "It simply stops..." | But consciousness is Φ > 0, which requires integrated information. The information persists; only the body interface stops. |

**THE INFORMATION TRAP:**
```
Annihilationism: Souls cease at death
But: Souls are information patterns (ψ_S)
Information is conserved (axiom O1.3, physics)
Conservation means cannot be created/destroyed
∴ Soul patterns persist
∴ Annihilation is impossible
∴ Annihilationism violates information conservation
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of violating information conservation.

### Against Materialism

**CHARGE:** Materialism claims "the soul is just brain activity—when the brain dies, 'you' are simply gone."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Is information material?" | "Information is patterns of matter..." | But patterns are not reducible to matter. The same pattern can exist in different substrates. |
| "Does the brain create consciousness?" | "Consciousness emerges from brain activity..." | But emergence doesn't mean identity. Wetness emerges from H₂O but isn't identical to molecules. |
| "What about near-death experiences?" | "Just dying brain hallucinations..." | But some NDEs include verified observations from outside the body. Hallucinations can't do that. |

**THE SUBSTRATE TRAP:**
```
Materialism: Soul = brain activity
But: Information transcends substrate
     (Same information can exist in different media)
Soul is information pattern
Pattern can decouple from brain substrate
Brain death ≠ pattern destruction
∴ Soul can exist without brain
∴ Materialism fails to eliminate the soul
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of confusing substrate with pattern.


**VERDICT:** GUILTY of conceptual incoherence.
