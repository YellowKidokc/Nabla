### 4. Self-Flip Impossibility (HM.4) — The Core Theorem

**No internal operation can change your own sign. This is the mathematical proof of *sola gratia*.**

| Type of Operation | Can It Flip σ? |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Self-effort | No—unitary operations preserve sign |
| Education | No—adds information, doesn't flip orientation |
| Therapy | No—adjusts behavior, doesn't flip sign |
| Meditation | No—may reveal sign, cannot flip it |
| Willpower | No—will is part of the σ = -1 system |

```
THEOREM (D1): Self-Flip Impossibility

Given:
1. σ ∈ {-1, +1} (binary moral sign)
2. Self-operations are unitary (information-preserving)
3. Unitary operations preserve eigenvalues

Then:
∀U_self: U_self|σ = -1⟩ ≠ |σ = +1⟩

Proof:
- Let U_self be any self-generated operation
- U_self is unitary (operates on self without external input)
- Unitary operations preserve eigenvalue spectrum
- σ is an eigenvalue of the moral orientation operator
- Therefore U_self preserves σ
- Therefore U_self|-1⟩ = |-1⟩ (possibly with phase)
- Self-flip is impossible ∎
```

**The bootstrap problem:**
```
You want to flip your σ
But "you" ARE the σ = -1 system
Your will, your effort, your choices all flow from σ = -1
You cannot get outside yourself to flip yourself
Like trying to lift yourself by your own bootstraps
```
