## What It Is

## The Measurement

"Precision" of a prophetic statement is operationalized as its information content: how many independent, testable, specific claims does it make? A prophecy that says "things will change" is low-precision. A prophecy that specifies a named king, a specific city, a time window, and a mechanism is high-precision.

The test scores prophetic statements across biblical eras for:
- Number of specific conditions
- Named entities (people, places)
- Time qualifications
- Mechanistic specificity (how the predicted event occurs)

The prediction: precision increases monotonically across eras, matching the informational complexity growth measured in Test 03.
