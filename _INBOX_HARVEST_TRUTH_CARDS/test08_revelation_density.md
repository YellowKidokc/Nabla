## The S-Curve Prediction

The chi-field's K (Information/Logos) component predicts that revelation density should follow a logistic growth curve over time:

> R(t) = R_max / (1 + exp(−k(t − t₀)))

Where:
- R_max is the maximum revelatory density (asymptote)
- k is the growth rate
- t₀ is the inflection point (halfway to maximum)

This is not an arbitrary choice of functional form. Logistic growth is the natural result of a system where growth is limited by a carrying capacity — revelation can only increase as fast as the community can receive, understand, and transmit it. The S-curve shape emerges from the interplay between growth pressure (K component) and saturation (finite human capacity).

The era sequence is fitted to a sigmoid curve. The quality of the sigmoid fit (R²) determines the verdict.
