# The New Scientific Method

> The current scientific method asks WHAT and HOW. It never formally asks WHY. That's the structural gap. This method closes it.

---

## Why the Traditional Method Fails

Three fatal weaknesses:

1. **Hypothesis Monoculture.** Usually only one hypothesis is tested. This creates confirmation bias. The method should require competing models.

2. **Statistical Worship.** P-values dominate reasoning, but statistics measure correlation strength, not truth. A p < 0.05 result can be completely wrong about mechanism.

3. **No "Why" Layer.** The method handles "what happens" and "how it happens." It systematically avoids "why it happens," "why it couldn't be otherwise," and "why these laws exist." This makes science purely descriptive. Description is not explanation.

### Historical Evidence

Every major breakthrough came from someone who asked why:

- **Kepler** — asked why orbits have structure. Got laws of planetary motion.
- **Newton** — asked why the cosmos obeys mathematical laws. Got universal gravitation.
- **Faraday** — asked why electricity and magnetism relate. Got field theory.
- **Maxwell** — asked why Faraday's fields behave as they do. Unified electromagnetism.
- **Planck** — asked why blackbody radiation didn't match predictions. Got quantum theory.
- **Lemaître** — asked why the universe appears to expand. Got the Big Bang.

Every dead framework refused to ask why:

- **Logical Positivism** — "Why is meaningless." Dead.
- **Behaviorism** — "Don't ask why people think." Dead.
- **Instrumentalism** — "Theories aren't about why." Stagnant.

The pattern is historically demonstrable. Asking why is the engine of discovery. Refusing it is the engine of stagnation.

## The New Method: 7 Questions Applied to Science

### Forward Direction (Investigate)

| Step | 7Q Question | Scientific Application |
|------|-------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **What is it?** | **Phenomenon Registration.** Define what needs explaining. Record observable event, measurement boundaries, signal vs noise, variables. Don't theorize yet. |
| 2 | **What domain is it in?** | **Structural Decomposition.** What entities exist? What relationships? What constraints? What conservation laws apply? Map structure before explanation. |
| 3 | **What is it claiming?** | **Competing Model Generation.** Generate ALL plausible models — not one hypothesis. Model A (deterministic), Model B (stochastic), Model C (emergent), Model D (artifact). Competition is mandatory. |
| 4 | **What supports it?** | **Prediction + Experiment + Data.** Each model must produce distinct testable predictions. Design experiments with isolation, sensitivity, replicability. Collect data without modifying the model. No retroactive hypothesis changes. |
| 5 | **What does it depend on?** | **Causal Depth — the WHY layer.** For each surviving model, ask: Why does this mechanism exist? Why couldn't it be otherwise? Why do these laws hold? Trace causes down to axioms or foundational rules. If you can't answer why, declare it an axiom and say so honestly. |
| 6 | **What does it affect or force?** | **Integration + Cross-Domain Coherence.** Does the surviving model unify multiple phenomena? Does it conflict with known constraints? Does it cohere across physics, mathematics, information theory, logic, and metaphysics? A model explaining physics should not contradict logic. |
| 7 | **What would kill it?** | **Falsification + Continuous Revision.** Every model carries its own kill conditions. A model dies if: predictions fail, it requires ad hoc adjustments, a competing model explains data better. No model becomes permanent truth. Science advances through model death, not confirmation. |

### Reverse Direction (Prove)

| Step | 7Q Reversed | Scientific Application |
|------|-------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **What would kill it?** | Start here. Define every possible way the claim could fail. Enumerate all death conditions. Try to destroy it. |
| 2 | **What does the survivor force?** | If it can't be killed, show what it necessarily produces. What predictions must follow? What other theories must be true? |
| 3 | **What does it depend on?** | Trace the foundations. What must already be true? What axioms? What laws? What assumptions? Expose the load-bearing walls. |
| 4 | **What supports it?** | NOW bring evidence. But only after survival is established. Evidence confirms what survived destruction — it doesn't lead. |
| 5 | **What is it claiming?** | State the claim. But by now it's a conclusion, not a hypothesis. The reader watched everything else die. |
| 6 | **What domain is it in?** | Show it holds across domains. Physics + mathematics + logic + information theory. If it only works in one domain, it might be local, not universal. |
| 7 | **What is it?** | Name it last. Identity earned through survival, not assumed at the start. |

## Q0: The Posture Before Inquiry

Before Q1, there is Q0. It is not a method step. It is a precondition.

**Q0: The inquirer cannot be the ground of inquiry. Arrive with humility, not with the answer.**

Every scientist listed above who asked why also held this posture — they believed the universe was intelligible because it was made by an intelligence greater than theirs. That belief wasn't incidental to their breakthroughs. It was the engine.

The traditional method has no equivalent of Q0. It assumes the investigator is a neutral observer. That assumption is false. Every investigator carries priors, and those priors shape what questions get asked. Q0 makes the priors explicit and demands openness to being wrong.

## The Three Types of "Why"

| Type | Question | Traditional Science | New Method |
|------|----------|-------------------|------------|
| Mechanistic | What process causes it? | ✅ Handles well | ✅ Q3-Q4 |
| Statistical | What probability structure produces the pattern? | ✅ Handles well | ✅ Q4 |
| Teleological | What purpose or goal explains it? | ❌ Avoids entirely | ✅ Q5 (Causal Depth) |
| Constraint | Why couldn't it be otherwise? | ⚠️ Occasionally (symmetry, conservation) | ✅ Q5 (forced) |
| Ontological | Why does this exist at all? | ❌ Labels as "philosophy" | ✅ Q5 → Q0 |

The new method formalizes all five types of why. The traditional method handles two and avoids three.

## Where Science Sits in the Full Framework

Science is one verification layer, not the whole system:

```
LEVEL 1: Logical coherence        ← Can this be stated without contradiction?
LEVEL 2: Metaphysical grounding   ← What must be true for this to hold?
LEVEL 3: Mathematical consistency ← Does the math work?
LEVEL 4: Empirical verification   ← Does reality agree?    ← SCIENCE LIVES HERE
LEVEL 5: Cross-domain coherence   ← Does it hold everywhere?
```

Science occupies Level 4. The traditional method acts as if Level 4 is the only level. The new method makes all five levels explicit.

## Death Conditions (How Models Die)

| Death Condition | Meaning | Example |
|----------------|---------|---------|
| Self-Refutation | The claim destroys itself | "There is no truth" is itself a truth claim |
| Infinite Regress | The explanation never bottoms out | "Who created the creator?" without terminal |
| Empirical Contradiction | Reality disagrees | Model predicts X, experiment shows Y |
| Logical Incoherence | Internal contradiction | Claiming both A and not-A |
| Collapses to Stronger Branch | Another model explains it better | Epicycles → Kepler |

The traditional method only uses Empirical Contradiction. The new method uses all five.

Instead of p-values alone, evaluate across:

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Predictive Accuracy | How well predictions match data |
| Parsimony | Model simplicity (Occam) |
| Explanatory Depth | Can it explain WHY, not just WHAT? |
| Robustness | Stability under perturbation |
| Falsifiability | Clear kill conditions exist |
| Cross-Domain Coherence | Holds across physics, math, logic, information |
| Causal Depth | Answers WHY, not just HOW |

## The Meta-Layer (Model Auditing)

Every field should periodically ask:

- What assumptions are hidden?
- What models are protected from falsification?
- What anomalies are being ignored?
- What questions are forbidden by institutional culture?

Many revolutions in science happened because anomalies were dismissed for decades. The meta-layer prevents this by making the audit mandatory.

## Where We Are Right

The historical pattern is demonstrable: scientists who asked why produced breakthroughs. The traditional method's avoidance of why is a structural gap, not a philosophical preference. The 7Q framework closes it.

## Where We Might Be Wrong

If Q5 (the why layer) is too unconstrained, it could admit speculative answers that feel explanatory but aren't testable. The constraint: every "why" answer must either explain the cause, explain the constraint that forces it, or declare itself a foundational axiom. No hiding.

## What We Think

The scientific method isn't broken at the logical level. It's broken at the institutional and philosophical level. The 7Q framework repairs both by adding mandatory model competition, five death conditions instead of one, a formal why layer, cross-domain coherence testing, and an epistemic precondition (Q0) that the traditional method lacks entirely.

Seven questions forward: investigate anything.
Seven questions reversed: prove anything.
Q0: arrive humble or don't arrive at all.

That is the new scientific method.
