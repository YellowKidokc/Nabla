## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Copenhagen Denial

**CHARGE:** Copenhagen Denial claims "there's no measurement problem—the math works, don't ask what it means."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "What causes wave function collapse?" | "The math just predicts probabilities..." | But the math has a discontinuity at measurement. What causes that discontinuity? |
| "Why does observation change the system?" | "It's just how the equations work..." | Equations describe; they don't explain. WHY do they work that way? |
| "Is Schrödinger's cat alive or dead before observation?" | "The question is meaningless..." | The cat has a real state. Avoiding the question doesn't dissolve it. |

**THE DISCONTINUITY TRAP:**
```
Copenhagen Denial: No need to interpret the math
But: The math has a discontinuity (collapse)
Discontinuities require explanation
"Shut up and calculate" isn't an explanation
∴ Something causes collapse
∴ Copenhagen Denial fails to engage the problem
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of philosophical evasion.

### Against Many-Worlds

**CHARGE:** Many-Worlds claims "there's no collapse—all possibilities exist in branching parallel universes."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

**THE PARSIMONY TRAP:**

This maximally violates Occam's Razor

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of ontological extravagance.

### Against Determinism

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of ignoring experimental evidence.
