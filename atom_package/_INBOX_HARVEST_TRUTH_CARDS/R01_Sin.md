## THE PROSECUTION

### Against "Sin is Social Construct"

**CHARGE:** Social Constructivism claims "sin is just what society disapproves of—it varies by culture, has no objective reality."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Is murder wrong only in some cultures?" | "Cultures define their own values..." | But all cultures prohibit random murder. There's a universal core. |
| "Is there any objective standard?" | "No, just social agreements..." | Then the statement 'there is no objective standard' is not objective either—just your culture's claim. |
| "Why do all societies have 'sin' categories?" | "Social cohesion..." | But why does social cohesion require prohibiting certain behaviors? Because those behaviors cause real damage. |

**THE UNIVERSAL CORE TRAP:**
```
Social Constructivism: Sin is culturally relative
But: All cultures prohibit: murder, theft, betrayal
This universal core suggests objective reality
Social construction cannot explain universal convergence
∴ Something objective grounds the convergence
∴ Sin is not merely social construct
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of ignoring universal moral constants.

### Against Moral Relativism

**CHARGE:** Moral Relativism claims "sin is relative—what's sin for you may not be sin for me."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Is coherence better than decoherence?" | "It depends on perspective..." | Decoherence destroys the system having the perspective. Death isn't a "perspective." |
| "Is it wrong for me to harm you?" | "Not absolutely..." | So if I harm you now, you have no complaint? |
| "Is moral relativism objectively true?" | "It's the correct view..." | If objectively true, then not everything is relative. Self-refuting. |

**THE SELF-REFUTATION TRAP:**
```
Moral Relativism: All moral claims are relative
But: "All moral claims are relative" claims to be non-relative
If relative, it doesn't bind those who reject it
If absolute, it refutes itself
∴ Moral relativism is incoherent
∴ Sin can be objectively defined
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of logical self-destruction.
