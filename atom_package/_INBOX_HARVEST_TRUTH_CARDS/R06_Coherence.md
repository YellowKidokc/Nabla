## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Annihilationism

**CHARGE:** Annihilationism claims "the unsaved are destroyed, cease to exist—no eternal conscious punishment."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "What happens to ψ_S at death?" | "It ceases to exist..." | But information cannot be destroyed (F02). ψ_S is information pattern. It persists in χ. |
| "Is non-existence a punishment?" | "Yes, loss of life..." | But you can't experience non-existence. Punishment requires an experiencer. Annihilation isn't punishment—it's escape. |
| "Why does Jesus speak of 'eternal punishment'?" | "Eternal means 'of the age'..." | But Matthew 25:46 uses the same word (αἰώνιος) for "eternal life" and "eternal punishment." Same duration. |

**THE INFORMATION TRAP:**
```
Annihilationism: The soul is destroyed
But: Information is conserved (F02)
ψ_S is information pattern in χ
Destruction of ψ_S would violate conservation
∴ ψ_S cannot be destroyed
∴ It persists—either in C_max (heaven) or C → 0 (hell)
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of denying information conservation.

---

### Against No Afterlife Views

**CHARGE:** No Afterlife views claim "death is the end—there is no continuation of consciousness."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "What happens to your Φ at death?" | "It ends..." | But Φ is integrated information. Where does the information go? Information is conserved. |
| "Is consciousness merely brain function?" | "Yes, it's neural..." | But brain is interface, not source (H02). Consciousness is ψ_S in χ, not just neurons. |
| "Why do humans universally sense an afterlife?" | "Wishful thinking..." | Universal intuition across all cultures points to something real. Complete error is unlikely. |

**THE INTERFACE TRAP:**
```
No Afterlife: Consciousness ends with brain death
But: Brain is interface to ψ_S (H02)
Interface death ≠ ψ_S death
Radio breaking ≠ broadcast ending
Brain death closes the interface; ψ_S continues in χ
∴ Afterlife is structurally required
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of confusing interface with source.

---

### Against Reincarnation

**CHARGE:** Reincarnation claims "the soul cycles through multiple lives—karma determines future incarnation."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Is identity preserved across lives?" | "The soul continues..." | But you don't remember past lives. If identity requires memory continuity (personal identity), how is it the same "you"? |
| "What is the goal of the cycle?" | "Escape from rebirth..." | But escape through effort contradicts self-flip impossibility (D1). You can't self-achieve liberation. |
| "Why no clear memory of past lives?" | "Memory is physical..." | If soul is non-physical but memory is physical, what is preserved? The soul without memory is not continuous identity. |

**THE IDENTITY TRAP:**
```
Reincarnation: Same soul in different bodies
But: Personal identity requires continuity
No memory continuity across lives
∴ No personal identity preserved
∴ "You" don't reincarnate—something does, but not "you"
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of dissolving personal identity.
