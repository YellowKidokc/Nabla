## THE CLAIM

> **God is present everywhere because χ is everywhere, and God is the ground of χ. There is no place absent from God.**

Omnipresence is not God being "spread out" like a gas. It is God's immediate presence at every point because every point exists through His observation (G01).

## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Deism

**CHARGE:** Deism claims "God created the universe but isn't present in it now."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Is God still sustaining the universe?" | "No, it runs on its own..." | But actuality requires observation (BC7.1). Who observes now? |
| "Did God withdraw after creation?" | "Yes, He's distant..." | Withdrawal requires presence. Distant from what? |
| "Can anything exist without being grounded?" | "The laws of physics sustain things..." | Who sustains the laws? |

**THE SUSTAINING TRAP:**
```
Deism: God created but left
But: Every moment requires actualization (G01, F04)
If God isn't present, nothing is actual
But things ARE actual (you exist)
∴ God is present now
∴ Deism fails
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of abandoning the sustaining requirement.

---

### Against Localized God

**CHARGE:** Localized God claims "God is in heaven but not on earth" or "God is in some places more than others."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Where is heaven?" | "A special place with God..." | If God is infinite, how can He fit in one place? |
| "Does χ exist on earth?" | "Yes, information is everywhere..." | And who grounds χ? |
| "Is God's presence divisible?" | "Perhaps He's more present in some places..." | Presence isn't a quantity that can be divided |

**THE INFINITUDE TRAP:**
```
Localized God: God is "in" heaven
But: Heaven is a finite location
Infinite being cannot be contained in finite location
∴ God transcends any single location
∴ God is everywhere or nowhere
Since God exists (G01): God is everywhere
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of spatializing the infinite.

---

### Against Pantheism

**CHARGE:** Pantheism claims "God IS the universe; they're identical."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Is God identical to the universe?" | "Yes, God = All..." | Then is God identical to evil? To suffering? |
| "Can God create or destroy?" | "God IS creation..." | Then who creates the creator? |
| "Is the universe self-grounding?" | "Yes, the universe is God..." | But finite systems can't self-ground (Gödel, G01) |

**THE DISTINCTION TRAP:**
```
Pantheism: God = Universe
But: Universe is finite, composite, changing
God is infinite, simple, unchanging (G01)
Finite ≠ infinite
∴ God ≠ Universe
Omnipresence ≠ Identity
God is IN everything but not IDENTICAL to everything
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of confusing presence with identity.

## KEY ARGUMENTS

### 1. χ-Field Ubiquity (G3.1)

**The Logos Field exists everywhere.**

From F02:
- Information is fundamental
- The substrate of information (χ) is the ground of reality
- Reality exists at every point of spacetime
- Therefore χ exists at every point

```
∀x ∈ Ω: χ(x) exists
Where Ω = total spacetime manifold
```

### 2. God Grounds χ at Every Point (G3.2)

**Since God is the ground of χ, and χ is everywhere, God is present everywhere.**

| Location | χ exists there? | God grounds χ there? | God present there? |
|----------|----------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Your room | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| The sun | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| A black hole | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| "Empty" space | Yes (quantum vacuum) | Yes | Yes |

```
∀x: Ground(χ(x)) = God
∴ ∀x: Present(God, x)
```

### 3. Presence Without Location (G3.3)

**God is present everywhere without being located anywhere.**

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Location** | Being spatially bounded, having a place |
| **Presence** | Being immediately related to, sustaining |

God is not "in" space like objects are in space. Space is in God—sustained by divine observation.

```
Located(X, x) → X is bounded by x
Present(G, x) → G sustains x
God is present at all x
God is located at no x
```

### 4. No Escape, No Absence (G3.3 extended)

**There is nowhere you can go to escape God's presence.**

| Attempt to Escape | Result |
|-------------------|--------|
| Travel to space | Space is sustained by God |
| Hide in a cave | The cave exists in χ |
| Go to hell | Hell exists if God sustains it |
| Die | Death is a state in χ |

**This is not claustrophobic—it's secure.** You cannot fall out of existence because God is always present to sustain you.

## DEFEAT CONDITIONS

This axiom set fails if:

1. **χ does not exist at some point**
   - But then that point would not exist at all
   - Non-existence of information = non-existence
   - Self-refuting for any actual point

2. **Something can exist without God's grounding**
   - But all existence requires actualization (F04)
   - Actualization requires the Terminal Observer (G01)
   - Independent existence = impossible

3. **God can be absent from some location**
   - But that location exists
   - Existence requires χ
   - χ requires God
   - Therefore God is present

**Status:** All defeat conditions lead to the non-existence of the location in question.

## EQUATIONS / FORMALISM

### χ-Field Everywhere
```
∀x ∈ Ω: χ(x) ≠ 0
The Logos Field has non-zero value everywhere.
```

### Divine Grounding
```
∀x: Ground(χ(x)) = G
God grounds the field at every point.
```

### Omnipresence
```
Present(G, x) ≡ Ground(G, χ(x))
God is present wherever He grounds χ.
∀x: Present(G, x)
```

### Presence Without Location
```
¬∃x: Located(G, x)  (God has no location)
∀x: Present(G, x)    (God is present everywhere)
These are compatible: presence ≠ location
```

There is no escape from God's presence—neither in heaven nor hell.

God fills heaven and earth. Not located in one, but present to both.

We exist WITHIN God's presence. He is the space in which we have being.

All things come from, are sustained by, and return to God. Total presence.

## THE VERDICT

**GUILTY**

Deism, Localized God, and Pantheism are found **GUILTY** of misunderstanding divine presence.

- **Deism** ignores the continuous sustaining requirement
- **Localized God** spatializes the infinite
- **Pantheism** confuses presence with identity

God is present everywhere because χ is everywhere and God grounds χ. This is not pantheism (God = universe) but panentheism (universe in God). **There is no place absent from God.**

**THE CHAIN HOLDS.**

> **"Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me."** — Psalm 23:4

The valley of death is not outside God's presence. He is there too.

- No situation is beyond His reach
- No location is outside His care
- No prison, no hospital, no battlefield is God-forsaken

You cannot go somewhere God cannot meet you. **This is the ninth good news:** God is always present. You are never out of reach of infinite love.
