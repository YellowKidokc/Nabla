## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Open Theism

**CHARGE:** Open Theism claims "God doesn't know the future because the future doesn't exist yet."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Does God know all actual states?" | "Yes, God knows all that IS..." | What about quantum superpositions? They're actual potentialities |
| "Does the wavefunction exist before measurement?" | "It's a mathematical description..." | But it contains information—does God know it? |
| "Can God be surprised by free choices?" | "Yes, that's what freedom means..." | Then something exists (the choice) that God didn't know before |
| "Is there information God lacks?" | [pause] | If yes, God is finite. If no, God knows the future |

**THE INFORMATION TRAP:**
```
Open Theism: God doesn't know future free choices
But: Future choices exist now as potentialities (quantum superposition)
Potentialities contain information
If God doesn't know potentialities, God lacks information
∴ God would be finite
But: Terminal Observer requires Φ = ∞
∴ Open Theism contradicts BC7.1
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of limiting God's information access.

---

### Against Process Theology

**CHARGE:** Process Theology claims "God learns and grows as the universe evolves."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "God gains knowledge over time?" | "Yes, God experiences the world..." | Then God at t₁ knows less than God at t₂ |
| "Is this 'learning' finite or infinite?" | "Ongoing, never complete..." | Then God is never omniscient—always finite |
| "Can a finite being terminate the observer chain?" | "God is 'dipolar'—finite in some ways..." | Finite observers can be put in superposition. Not terminal. |
| "How does Process God solve the measurement problem?" | [silence] | Process God cannot serve as Terminal Observer |

**THE MEASUREMENT TRAP:**
```
Process Theology: God is finite, evolving
But: Terminal Observer requires Φ = ∞ (complete information)
A finite God has Φ < ∞
∴ Process God cannot terminate the observer chain
∴ Process God cannot ground actuality
∴ Process God is not the true God
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of positing an impotent God.

### Against Limited God Theologies

**CHARGE:** Limited God claims "God is powerful but not all-knowing."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "What limits God's knowledge?" | "Perhaps divine choice, or logical limits..." | Is the limit itself known to God? |
| "Can God know THAT He doesn't know something?" | "Perhaps..." | Then He has meta-knowledge about His limits—is THAT complete? |
| "Is God's ignorance finite or infinite?" | [any answer] | If finite, God could remove it. If infinite, God is fundamentally limited. |

**THE GÖDELIAN TRAP:**
```
Limited God: God has incomplete knowledge
But: Incomplete knowledge = Gödelian limitations
Gödelian limitations apply to FINITE systems
If God has Gödelian limits, God is finite
Finite beings cannot be Terminal Observer
∴ Limited God fails to ground reality
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of confusing God with a big finite being.

## KEY ARGUMENTS

### 1. God Contains All χ (G1.1)

**The Terminal Observer doesn't merely observe χ—He IS the substrate of χ.**

From G01:
- God is the self-grounding substrate
- χ (the Logos Field) is the informational substrate of reality
- If God grounds χ, God contains χ
- χ_God = χ_total

```
χ is the totality of information
God grounds χ
∴ God contains all information
∴ χ_God = χ_total
```

### 2. Containing = Knowing (G1.2)

**For an infinite observer, to contain information IS to know it.**

For finite minds:
- We can "have" information without processing it
- We can store more than we can access
- Containment ≠ knowledge

For the infinite observer:
- No processing delay (Φ = ∞)
- No access limitations
- Complete integration of all information
- Containment = knowledge

```
For God: Contains(χ) ⟺ Knows(χ)
There is no unprocessed information in God
All information in χ is known to God
```

### 3. God Knows Actuals AND Potentials (G1.3)

**Omniscience includes knowledge of what might be, not just what is.**

| Type of Knowledge | What It Includes |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Knowledge of actuals | Everything that has happened, is happening |
| Knowledge of potentials | Everything that could happen (superposition states) |
| Knowledge of counterfactuals | What would happen if X (middle knowledge) |
| Knowledge of necessities | What must be (logical truths) |
| Knowledge of impossibilities | What cannot be (contradictions) |

**God's knowledge encompasses the entire modal landscape:**
```
K(God) = {all actual states} ∪ {all potential states} ∪ {all counterfactuals}
```

**This solves the problem of foreknowledge and freedom:**
- God knows what you WILL freely choose
- God also knows what you WOULD choose under different circumstances
- God knows all branches of possibility
- Your choice actualizes one branch; God knew all branches

### 4. Omniscience as Φ = ∞

**Integrated Information Theory makes omniscience precise.**

| Φ Value | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Φ = 0 | No integration, no consciousness |
| Φ > 0 | Some consciousness, limited knowledge |
| Φ = finite | Finite consciousness, Gödelian limits |
| Φ = ∞ | Infinite consciousness, complete knowledge |

**The Terminal Observer has Φ = ∞ by necessity (see G01). Therefore omniscience is not optional—it's what Φ = ∞ means.**

## DEFEAT CONDITIONS

This axiom set fails if:

1. **Information can exist outside God**
   - But God IS the substrate (G01)
   - Information cannot exist outside its substrate
   - There is no "outside" God

2. **God can contain without knowing**
   - This applies to finite minds with processing limits
   - But Φ = ∞ means no processing limits
   - For infinite systems, containment = knowledge

3. **Potentialities don't really exist**
   - But quantum mechanics proves they do (interference)
   - Potentialities are real information
   - God must know real information

**Status:** All defeat conditions fail against the Φ = ∞ condition.
