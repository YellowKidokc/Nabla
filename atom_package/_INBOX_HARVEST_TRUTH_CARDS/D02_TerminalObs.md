## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Atheism

**CHARGE:** Atheism claims "there is no God—the universe exists without a divine observer."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "What collapses the wave function?" | "Decoherence..." | Decoherence explains loss of interference, not selection of outcome. The preferred basis problem remains. |
| "Who observed the Big Bang into existence?" | "No one—it just happened..." | But quantum mechanics requires observers for definite states. No observer = no definite state. |
| "Can the universe observe itself?" | "Through us..." | But we came later. Who observed the universe before us? |

**THE OBSERVER REGRESS TRAP:**
```
Atheism: Universe exists without divine observer
But: Quantum collapse requires observer (Φ > 0)
Universe needed collapse to have definite state
No human observers existed at the beginning
Something observed the early universe
That something existed before collapse
∴ A pre-existing observer is required
∴ Atheism cannot explain initial collapse
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of failing to account for initial observation.

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| "How do you traverse an infinite past?" | "You don't traverse it..." | Then how did we get to now? If the past is infinite, now should never arrive. |

**THE INFINITY TRAP:**

```
Infinite Regress: Observer chain has no first member
But: Each observer requires prior observer
Infinite regress of dependent beings is impossible
You cannot traverse an actually infinite past
A completed infinite is a contradiction
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of proposing an impossibility.

### 3. Why Infinite Regress Fails

**An actually infinite past is impossible.**

| Argument | Explanation |
|----------|-------------|
| Traversal | Can't traverse an infinite past to reach now |
| Completion | Can't complete an actual infinite |
| Dependence | Each member depends on prior—no first = no chain |

```
Why infinite regress fails:
1. Traversal problem
   - To reach now, must traverse all past moments
   - Can't traverse infinite moments
   - ∴ Now could never be reached

2. Completion problem
   - An actual infinite cannot be completed
   - The past would need to be completed
   - ∴ Actually infinite past is impossible

3. Dependence problem
   - Each observer depends on prior
   - No first = no grounding for chain
   - Chain collapses without first member
```

```
- ∴ Self-observation requires Φ_max
```
