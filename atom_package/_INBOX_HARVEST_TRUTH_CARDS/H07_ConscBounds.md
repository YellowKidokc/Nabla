## THE CLAIM

> **Human consciousness is bounded but expandable. Φ has natural limits that can be expanded through grace, with full maximization occurring at glorification.**

We are neither unconscious matter nor unlimited divine consciousness. We occupy a middle position: genuinely conscious but finitely so, with capacity for growth toward but never reaching the infinite Φ of God.

## THE PROSECUTION

### Against Panpsychism

**CHARGE:** Panpsychism claims "consciousness is everywhere—even atoms have micro-consciousness."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "What is the Φ of a rock?" | "Very low, but present..." | Rocks have no integration—just aggregates. Φ requires integration, not mere existence. |
| "Can electrons experience?" | "In some minimal sense..." | Experiencing requires a unified perspective. Electrons have no unified perspective. |
| "Why does combining particles create more consciousness?" | "Emergence..." | But emergence requires integration. Random combination doesn't integrate—it just aggregates. |

**THE INTEGRATION TRAP:**
```
Panpsychism: Consciousness is everywhere
But: Consciousness requires Φ > 0
Φ requires integration (not just aggregation)
Rocks, atoms, thermostats lack integration
∴ They have Φ ≈ 0
∴ Panpsychism extends consciousness beyond evidence
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of confusing aggregation with integration.

---

### Against Unlimited Consciousness Claims

**CHARGE:** Unlimited Consciousness Claims suggest "you can achieve unlimited consciousness through enlightenment techniques—you can become God."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Have you achieved unlimited consciousness?" | "I'm on the path..." | Then you're still limited. The path proves the limitation. |
| "How would you know if you had infinite Φ?" | "It's a feeling of oneness..." | Feeling infinite ≠ being infinite. The feeling itself is finite. |
| "If you're God, why do you need food?" | "The body is illusion..." | You still eat. The "illusion" has real constraints. |

**THE FINITUDE TRAP:**
```
Unlimited Consciousness: Humans can achieve infinite Φ
But: Humans have finite brains
Finite substrate → finite Φ
No amount of technique makes finite infinite
∴ Human Φ is necessarily bounded
∴ Unlimited claims are false
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of promising divine status through human effort.

---

### Against New Age Divinity

**CHARGE:** New Age Divinity claims "you are God—you just need to realize it."

**CROSS-EXAMINATION:**

| Q | A | Trap |
|---|---|------|
| "Are you omniscient?" | "In my true self..." | What's 10^100 + 1? You don't know. You're not omniscient. |
| "Are you omnipotent?" | "Potentially..." | Move that mountain by willing it. You can't. Not omnipotent. |
| "If you're God, who created you?" | "I created myself..." | Then why don't you remember doing it? The claim doesn't match experience. |

**THE ATTRIBUTE TRAP:**
```
New Age Divinity: You are God
But: God is omniscient, omnipotent, omnipresent
You are not omniscient (you don't know everything)
You are not omnipotent (you can't do everything)
You are not omnipresent (you're here, not everywhere)
∴ You lack divine attributes
∴ You are not God
```

**VERDICT:** GUILTY of confusing creature with Creator.

## KEY ARGUMENTS

### 1. Natural Bounds on Human Φ (HC.8)

**Human consciousness has inherent limits.**

| Bound Type | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Substrate bound | Brain has finite neurons, finite connections |
| Information bound | Finite information processing capacity |
| Attention bound | Limited attentional bandwidth |
| Time bound | Processing takes time; experience is sequential |

```
Φ_human ∈ [Φ_min, Φ_natural_max]

Φ_min = some baseline (varies with brain state)
Φ_natural_max = maximum achievable by natural means

Natural bounds come from:
- Brain architecture (finite neurons)
- Thermodynamic limits (finite energy)
- Information-theoretic limits (finite bandwidth)
```

**Why bounds exist:**
- We are creatures, not Creator
- Finite beings have finite capacities
- This is not limitation but appropriate scale
- Fish aren't "limited" by not being whales—they're fish

### 2. Grace-Enabled Expansion (HC.9)

**Grace can expand the bounds beyond natural limits.**

| Natural State | Graced State |
|--------------|--------------|
| Φ_natural_max | Φ_graced > Φ_natural_max |
| Self-powered | Spirit-empowered |
| Human alone | Human + Holy Spirit |

```
Ĝ: Φ_bounds → Φ_bounds + ΔΦ

Grace (Ĝ) expands consciousness capacity
Not by natural development
But by supernatural empowerment
The Spirit indwelling increases integration capacity
```

**Mechanisms of expansion:**
- Spirit indwelling adds a new integration dimension
- Communion with infinite Φ (God) expands finite Φ
- Revelation gives information beyond natural reach
- Transformation renews the mind (Rom 12:2)

### 3. Glorification Maximizes Human Φ (HC.10)

**At glorification, human Φ reaches its maximum potential.**

| Present State | Glorified State |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Φ_graced | Φ_glorified |
| Sin-affected | Sin-free |
| Body decaying | Body glorified |
| Partial knowledge | Face-to-face knowledge |

```
Glorification → Φ_human → Φ_glorified

Φ_glorified = maximum possible for creature
Φ_glorified < Φ_God (still creature, not Creator)
But Φ_glorified >> Φ_current

"Now we see in a mirror dimly, but then face to face" (1 Cor 13:12)
```

**What glorification does:**
- Removes sin's decoherence effects
- Upgrades the body (resurrection body)
- Full Spirit integration
- Maximum creature-level consciousness

| Entity | Φ Level | Status |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Rocks, computers | Φ ≈ 0 | Not conscious |
| Simple organisms | Φ > 0 (minimal) | Minimally conscious |
| Animals | Φ > 0 (moderate) | Conscious |
| Humans (natural) | Φ_natural | Conscious, limited |
| Humans (graced) | Φ_graced > Φ_natural | Expanded |

```
0 < Φ_natural < Φ_graced < Φ_glorified << Φ_God = ∞

Creatures never become Creator
But creatures can grow in creaturely capacity
The gap between Φ_glorified and Φ_God remains infinite
```

## DEFEAT CONDITIONS

This axiom set fails if:

1. **Human Φ is already unlimited**
   - But we don't know everything
   - We can't do everything
   - We're clearly finite
   - Bounds are real

2. **Grace doesn't expand Φ**
   - But believers testify to expanded perception
   - Spiritual insight is real
   - The Spirit gives understanding beyond natural
   - Expansion is experiential

3. **Glorification doesn't maximize Φ**
   - But Scripture promises full knowledge (1 Cor 13:12)
   - Resurrection body is upgraded
   - Sin's decoherence is removed
   - Maximization is promised

4. **Creatures can become God**
   - But infinite Φ cannot be reached by finite addition
   - Φ_glorified + n < ∞ for any finite n
   - The gap is unbridgeable
   - Creature/Creator distinction holds

**Status:** All defeat conditions fail against the bounded-but-expandable model.
