## Ruling

The four-layer stack survives the adversarial read, but only in repaired form.

## Direction-Stamped Rule

Lower layers may constrain higher-layer interpretation.

Higher layers may interpret lower-layer facts.

But:

- lower layers do not derive higher-layer meaning by themselves;
- higher layers do not override lower-layer jurisdiction.

## Legal Moves

- L1 physics constrains what L2 information grammar can claim.
- L2 information grammar constrains what L3 consciousness claims can claim.
- L3 observer/agency boundaries constrain what L4 theology must account for.
- L4 theology interprets the total stack as Author/source/purpose.

## Illegal Moves

- L1 physics proves L4 doctrine.
- L4 theology forces L1 data to mean what native physics does not support.
- L2 grammar silently upgrades analogy to identity.
- L3 consciousness claims become proof of theology without bridge grade.

