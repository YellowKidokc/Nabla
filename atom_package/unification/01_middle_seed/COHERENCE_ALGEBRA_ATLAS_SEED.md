## Purpose

This seed defines the Coherence-Degradation-Restoration grammar as the reusable L2 bridge structure for the Faith Through Physics framework.

It exists to prevent two opposite errors:

1. Reducing every domain's use of coherence into the same local meaning.
2. Treating recurring coherence/decoherence/restoration structures as accidental verbal similarity.

The correct middle is structural instantiation.

## Core Grammar

For any domain `D`, define:

```text
CDR(D) = <C, Delta, M, T, A, R, X>
```

where:

- `C` = coherence state
- `Delta` = degradation, decoherence, disorder, fragmentation, or failure state
- `M` = measurement signs
- `T` = threshold or phase-change condition
- `A` = asymmetry marker
- `R` = restoration mechanism
- `X` = counterexample, inversion, or local-disorder case

## Canonical Claim

Distinct domains can instantiate a shared asymmetric ordering algebra while preserving their native measurements, mechanisms, and limits.

## Rejected Claim

All uses of the word "coherence" are identical.
