## Red / Needs Guardrails

### 1. The Title “Math Is God”

Problem:

- Too easy to misread as pantheism or category collapse.
- It risks saying mathematics is identical to God rather than mathematics is an intelligible witness to created order.

Better titles:

- `Mathematics as Witness to Logos`
- `The Logos Identity Claim`
- `The Equations Were Never Secular`
- `The Rosetta Stone of Theophysics`

### 2. Quantum Observer Claims

Problem:

- “Consciousness itself collapses wavefunctions” is not established physics.
- The no-communication/measurement guardrails must be applied before any public version.

Allowed rewrite:

> The measurement problem leaves open interpretive questions about observation, information, and physical records. The framework treats consciousness-coupling as a hypothesis, not as a settled result of quantum mechanics.

### 3. Empirical Sigma Claims

Problem:

- GCP, PEAR, prophecy-correlation numbers must be source-gated before they can support a claim.

Allowed rewrite:

> Anomalous consciousness-correlation datasets may be examined as candidate evidence, but they do not become load-bearing until the sources, methods, and rival explanations are reviewed.

### 4. Raw “X IS Y” Identity Language

Problem:

- “Sin IS entropy,” “faith IS collapse,” “worship IS resonance,” and similar statements are rhetorically powerful but not automatically canon-safe.

Rule:

> Identity language is allowed only after bridge grading. Until then, render as “may disclose the same structure,” “functions as a structural grammar for,” or “is proposed as the theological naming of.”
