# PART 1: THE PRINCIPLES

## What This Is

This is the canonical folder architecture, content pipeline, website 
specification, and rigor framework for the entire Theophysics Research 
Initiative. Every AI collaborator, every human contributor, every 
piece of content follows this structure.

This document governs: folder organization, content classification, 
file tagging, route decisions, website page types, rigor requirements, 
and the non-negotiable descent rule.

Read it before you do anything else.

---

## The Three Laws

### LAW 1: DOMAINS ARE THE SKELETON
The top level is knowledge domains. Physics, theology, psychology, 
economics, education — each gets its own folder. The list is infinite. 
New domain = new folder. The internal structure is always the same.

### LAW 2: THE ARC IS THE HEARTBEAT
Inside every domain is a 14-stage arc representing the complete 
lifecycle of a truth-claim: from raw idea to confirmed reality. 
Not every piece of content runs all 14 stages. The stages exist 
for every domain. The ones that complete the full arc are the 
strongest pieces in the framework.

### LAW 3: DOWN IS NEVER OPTIONAL
Content can enter at any stage. It can skip every stage ABOVE its 
entry point. It can NEVER skip the stages below it. Every truth 
must descend until it reaches the everyday person.

The PhD paper that never becomes a Monday morning conversation is 
a failed paper. The equation that never becomes "here's what this 
means for your marriage" hasn't finished its job.

The everyday person is the floor. Everything reaches the floor.

Truth always flows down. That's the ministry. The structure enforces it.

# PART 2: THE 14-STAGE ARC

Every domain folder contains these subfolders. They are numbered 
so they sort correctly in every file browser and Obsidian vault.

```
[domain]\
├── 00_inbox_working\       ← Raw thinking, voice dumps, unsorted
├── 01_canonical\           ← Locked truth, proven claims
├── 02_paradigm\            ← What this breaks, new mental model
├── 03_synthesis\           ← How it connects to other domains
├── 04_hypothesis\          ← If true, then THIS must follow
├── 05_evidence\            ← External research, LLM, wiki, data
├── 06_falsification\       ← Kill conditions, what would break it
├── 07_paper\               ← Formal doctoral-level treatment
├── 08_objections\          ← Strongest pushback, steelmanned
├── 09_everyday\            ← Plain language Monday morning version
├── 10_worldcheck\          ← Everyday version pressure-tested
├── 11_articles\            ← Narrative story form, readable
├── 12_audience\            ← SEO, social, widest reach, toolkits
└── 13_fulfilled\           ← Did it hold, what happened, the receipt
```

## Stage 00: inbox_working — "I'm thinking about this"

The raw intake. Voice-to-text dumps, session notes, half-formed 
ideas, links, research pulls. Nothing is organized here. This is 
the messy creative space where insight begins.

**Contents:** Voice captures, session logs, AI conversation exports, 
raw research dumps, "what if" notes, anything unclassified.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
1. Research provenance — where did this idea originate?
2. Date-stamped ideation trail — when did we first think of this?
3. Source attribution — who contributed (which AI, which session)?
4. Raw data preservation — original voice captures, unedited

**Exit criteria:** Can you state the claim in one sentence? Then move it.

**Route class:** Raw/unsorted. No descent obligation yet.

**Website:** Not public. Internal workspace only.

## Stage 01: canonical — "This is what we know for sure"

Locked truth. Claims that have survived /PROBE, /EAST, /CHAIN and 
David has approved. Doesn't change without extraordinary reason.

**Contents:** Formal claim statements, derivation chains, mathematical 
formalizations, domain thesis statements, domain control sheets/indexes.

**Rigor satisfied (6):**
5. Formal claim statement — precise, unambiguous
6. Derivation chain — step-by-step from axiom to conclusion
7. Mathematical formalization — equations, not just words
8. Axiom dependency map — which axioms this rests on
9. Internal consistency proof — no contradiction with other canonical claims
10. Version history — how the claim evolved before locking

**Exit criteria:** Survived /PROBE and /EAST from 2+ AI collaborators. 
David reviewed. No contradiction with existing canonical claims.

**Descent obligation:** MUST eventually produce 09_everyday at minimum.

**Route class:** Canonical Control. Versioned, dependency-tracked, claim IDs.

**Website page type:** Domain Page (overview + route status) and 
Claim Page (stable claim with status, tests, updates).

## Stage 02: paradigm — "Here's why you have to think differently"

The mental model replacement. Demolish the old way of thinking, 
install the new one. Without this, readers process new information 
through their old paradigm and it bounces off.

**Contents:** Every paradigm document answers three questions:
1. What does everyone currently believe? (the old paradigm)
2. What does our work show instead? (the break)
3. How do you need to think differently now? (the new paradigm)

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
11. Prior paradigm documentation — what did people believe before?
12. Explicit break statement — exactly what changes and why
13. Mental model replacement — the new way, not just critique
14. Historical precedent — other paradigm shifts with this pattern

**Route class:** Framework claim. Requires descent through all lower stages.

**Website page type:** Embedded in Domain Page or standalone Paradigm Page.

---

## Stage 03: synthesis — "Here's how it connects to everything else"

Cross-domain bridges. The isomorphisms live here. Same equation, 
same structure, same pattern across domains that shouldn't relate.

**Contents:** Formal isomorphism proofs (not analogies — actual shared 
math), cross-domain mappings, Master Equation connections, Law 
connections, structural correspondence documents.

**Rigor satisfied (5):**
15. Cross-domain mapping — formal isomorphism to other domains
16. Structural correspondence proof — shared math, not metaphor
17. Bidirectional prediction — mapping works both directions
18. Domain boundary conditions — where it holds and breaks
19. Integration with Master Equation — connection to χ

**Route class:** Framework claim. Requires descent.

**Website page type:** Claim Page with cross-domain links.

---

## Stage 04: hypothesis — "If true, then THIS must follow"

Predictions. Where Theophysics becomes science instead of philosophy.

**Contents:** Testable predictions with expected outcomes, predicted 
magnitudes, test methodology proposals, confidence levels, 
derived-from traces.

**Rigor satisfied (5):**
20. Testable prediction — specific, measurable, falsifiable
21. Predicted magnitude — not just direction but size
22. Test methodology — how you WOULD test this
23. Confidence level — how sure before testing
24. Derived-from trace — which canonical claim generates this

**Route class:** Framework claim. Requires descent.

**Website page type:** Claim Page (prediction variant).

---

## Stage 05: evidence — "Here's what the world's research says"

External evidence. NOT our work — the world's work, gathered to 
check our claims against reality.

**Contents:** Published academic papers, LLM research pulls, 
Wikipedia-level baselines, statistical data, competing theories, 
data sets.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
25. External validation survey — what does mainstream science say?
26. Competing framework comparison — how do alternatives explain this?
27. LLM/AI consensus check — what do models say unprompted?
28. Raw data availability — source data preserved and accessible

**Route class:** Evidence Pack. Source verification required, then 
lower stages once tied to a claim.

**Website page type:** Evidence Page (data, sources, external notes). 
Must distinguish source notes from conclusions.

---

## Stage 06: falsification — "What would break it, and we tried"

Kill conditions. Documented attempts to destroy the claim. 
Intellectual honesty lives here.

**Contents:** Kill attempts and results, strongest counter-arguments 
(/EAST — steelmanned), boundary conditions, failure modes, 
survival documentation.

**Rigor satisfied (5):**
29. Kill attempts — documented destruction attempts
30. Strongest counter-argument — the /EAST that almost won
31. Boundary conditions — where the claim stops holding
32. Failure modes — what WOULD falsify this
33. Survival documentation — why it survived

**When required:** Use falsification when the piece claims cross-domain 
validity, scientific structure, predictive power, formal necessity, 
framework proof, or broad causal mechanism. Do NOT force on devotional 
writing, practical ministry, templates, simple reflections, audience 
copy, or story-first pieces not making formal claims.

**Route class:** Framework claim component.

**Website page type:** Integrated into Claim Page as falsification section.

## Stage 07: paper — "The formal doctoral-level treatment"

The academic paper. Built FROM stages 01-06. The version a PhD 
reviewer reads.

**Contents — the doctoral template:**
1. Title
2. Abstract (problem, claim, method, result, limitation)
3. Core Claim (exactly what is being argued)
4. Scope (what this covers and what it does NOT)
5. Definitions (key terms, variables, laws, domain meanings)
6. Prior Work / Existing Frame
7. Paradigm Shift (what assumption is being challenged)
8. Argument Chain (step-by-step reasoning)
9. Evidence (internal + external + data + source contact)
10. Falsification / Kill Conditions
11. Objections (strongest first)
12. Conclusion (what holds, uncertain, what to test next)
13. **Everyday Bridge** (link back to plain-language version)

**Rigor satisfied (5):**
34. Formal academic structure — follows doctoral template
35. Complete argument chain — step-by-step, no gaps
36. Citation framework — sources identified and accessible
37. Scope limitation — explicitly states what it doesn't cover
38. Everyday bridge — paper links BACK to the plain version

**THE RULE:** The last section of every paper is "Everyday Bridge." 
The paper does not exist in isolation from the people it serves.

**Route class:** Full Framework Claim. Requires descent through ALL 
lower stages: 08 → 09 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13.

**Website page type:** Paper Page (formal treatment).

## Stage 08: objections — "The strongest pushback, steelmanned"

Not strawmen. The real objections that make serious physicists or 
theologians pause. Articulated as well as their best advocates would. 
Then answered.

**Contents:** Steelmanned objections (/EAST), point-by-point responses, 
unresolved objections (intellectual honesty), academic critiques, 
common misunderstandings.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
39. Steelmanned objections — strongest form, not strawmen
40. Point-by-point responses — each objection answered
41. Unresolved items documented — intellectual honesty
42. Source attribution — who raised this objection

**Route class:** Framework claim component.

**Website page type:** Objections Page (separate from paper body).

---

## Stage 09: everyday — "Here's what it means in plain English"

The Monday morning version. No equations. No jargon. No framework 
vocabulary. Written so your neighbor, your barber, your aunt can 
understand what you found and why it matters to THEIR life.

**Contents:** Plain language translation, practical application, 
analogies and metaphors, "so what?" answer.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
43. Plain language translation — a non-expert can understand
44. Practical application — what this means for daily life
45. Analogy/metaphor — accessible entry point
46. "So what?" test — why should anyone care

**THE RULE:** This is the FLOOR for canonical claims. Every canonical 
truth must eventually produce an everyday version. If it can't be 
explained in plain language, the idea isn't clear enough yet.

**Route class:** Everyday/Ministry. Requires 12_audience if publishing.

**Website page type:** Everyday Page (plain-language version). Every 
public page must have a "plain version" link or TODO marker. Every 
formal paper must have an everyday companion page.

---

## Stage 10: worldcheck — "Does the plain version survive reality?"

The everyday translation pressure-tested against what's actually out 
there. Real reactions, mainstream framing, fact-checking.

**Contents:** Real-world reactions, mainstream media framing differences, 
simplification audit (too simple? too complex?), social media responses, 
fact-check results.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
47. Real-world pressure test — everyday vs actual reactions
48. Simplification audit — anything lost in translation?
49. Cultural framing check — how mainstream frames this differently
50. Fact-check verification — does the plain version hold up?

**Route class:** Quality gate before articles.

**Website page type:** Worldcheck Page (outside contact and pressure test).

---

## Stage 11: articles — "The story version, readable and human"

Resynthesized narrative treatment. After everyday survives worldcheck, 
retold as story. Theopoetic Engine: Human Anchor → Principle Revealed 
→ Jesus Story → Core Insight → Truth Bomb. Above high school, below PhD.

**Contents:** Series articles (GTQ, CNS, CDT, DRV, Logos Papers), 
standalone narrative treatments, long-form readable pieces, 
cross-references to related domain articles.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
51. Multi-level explanation — above-HS, below-PhD accessible
52. Narrative structure — human story anchoring the concept
53. Cross-references — links to related articles
54. Reading level calibration — accessible but not dumbed down

**Route class:** Article Treatment. Requires 12_audience descent.

**Website page type:** Article Page (readable treatment). Must link 
upward to rigor without forcing readers into it. Must link downward 
to everyday/audience versions.

---

## Stage 12: audience — "The version that reaches everyone"

SEO, social media, short-form, shareable, toolkits. The widest reach. 
Truth meets the person who would never read an article but might see 
a 30-second clip that stops their scroll.

**Contents:** SEO-optimized versions, social media posts/threads, 
short-form video scripts, infographics, one-pagers, podcast outlines, 
operational toolkits (FOIA templates, opt-out letters, action guides).

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
55. Impact assessment — how does this change the listener's world?
56. Accessibility verification — no barrier to entry
57. Actionability — audience can DO something with this
58. Shareability — formatted for platforms people actually use

**Route class:** Audience/Toolkit. Toolkit pages carry verification 
warnings when legal, medical, financial, or policy claims can change.

**Website page type:** Audience Page (social, SEO, teaching, action) 
and Toolkit Page (templates, guides, public action).

---

## Stage 13: fulfilled — "What actually happened"

The receipt. The honest ledger. Predictions confirmed, partially 
confirmed, or failed. Real-world results.

**Contents:** Confirmed predictions (with data), partial confirmations, 
failed predictions (JUST AS IMPORTANT), experimental results, 
real-world outcomes, revision triggers.

**Rigor satisfied (4):**
59. Experimental results — actual data from tests
60. Prediction accuracy — did hypothesis match reality?
61. Failed predictions logged — intellectual honesty ledger
62. Revision triggers — what needs updating based on results?

**THE LOOP:** Confirmed predictions strengthen 01_canonical. Failed 
predictions trigger new entries in 00_inbox_working. The arc is a 
cycle, not a line.

**Route class:** Tracking/outcome.

**Website page type:** Fulfilled Page (outcome and revision tracking). 
Tracks what held, failed, partially held, or needs revision.

# PART 3: ROUTE CLASSIFICATION

## The Route Decision Tree

Ask these questions in order for any file:

1. Is it raw, incomplete, or unsorted? → **00_inbox_working**
2. Is it a source pack, spreadsheet, data note, or research extract? → **05_evidence**
3. Is it a formal framework claim needing proof, definitions, evidence, 
   falsification, and objections? → Route upward toward **07_paper**
4. Is it a developed readable treatment? → **11_articles**
5. Is it plain-language ministry, practical teaching, or direct 
   application? → **09_everyday**
6. Is it a template, checklist, action guide, SEO plan, or 
   distribution asset? → **12_audience**
7. Is it tracking whether a claim held up? → **13_fulfilled**

## Route Classes

| Class | Entry Point | Required Descent | Example |
|-------|------------|-----------------|---------|
| Full Framework Claim | 01-07 | ALL lower stages | Education transmission failure |
| Article Treatment | 11_articles | 12_audience + 13_fulfilled | Accessible domain essay |
| Everyday/Ministry | 09_everyday | 12_audience if publishing | Jesus/fishermen teaching |
| Evidence Pack | 05_evidence | Lower stages once tied to claim | Gemini deep research |
| Toolkit | 12_audience | Factual/legal check + 13_fulfilled | Parent FOIA templates |
| Canonical Control | 01_canonical | Version control + descent to 09 | Axiom/domain index |

## When Falsification Is Required

**USE** falsification when the piece claims: cross-domain validity, 
scientific structure, predictive power, formal necessity, framework 
proof, broad causal mechanism.

**DO NOT FORCE** on: devotional writing, practical ministry, templates, 
simple reflections, audience copy, story-first pieces not making 
formal claims.

## The Default Entry Point

Most mature content enters at **11_articles** — "here's what we found 
and why it matters." Above everyday, below PhD. The natural gravity.

Content entering HIGH (PhD, proofs): starts at 07_paper or 01_canonical, 
gets pushed DOWN toward 09_everyday and 12_audience.

Content entering MIDDLE (most articles): starts at 11_articles, can 
push UP if claims warrant, MUST push DOWN to 12_audience.

Content entering LOW (ministry, toolkits): starts at 09_everyday or 
12_audience. May never need higher. THAT'S FINE.

# PART 4: THE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE

## PUSHING UP IS OPTIONAL. PUSHING DOWN IS NEVER OPTIONAL.

Every piece of content can skip stages ABOVE its entry point.
No piece of content can skip stages BELOW its entry point.

```
ENTRY AT 01_canonical:
  MUST produce → 09_everyday (at minimum)
  SHOULD produce → 11_articles, 12_audience

ENTRY AT 07_paper:
  MUST produce → 09_everyday, 11_articles, 12_audience

ENTRY AT 11_articles:
  MUST produce → 12_audience

ENTRY AT 09_everyday:
  MUST produce → 12_audience if publishing

ENTRY AT 12_audience:
  Already at the floor. Job done for reach.
  CAN push up if content warrants deeper treatment.
```

Folders above entry point = OPTIONAL (push up if it warrants)
Folders below entry point = YOUR TODO LIST (non-negotiable)

Empty upper layers: labeled "not needed yet" or "not elevated."
Empty lower layers: labeled **"unfinished descent."**

The framework is not broken by pieces that skip upper stages.
The framework IS tested by the pieces that take the full route.
Every piece still flows down to people.
That is the ministry rule inside the architecture.

# PART 5: FILE TAGGING

## The Three-Field Tag

Every file gets three tags in its frontmatter:

```yaml
---
title: "The Transmission Failure"
domain: education
entry_layer: 07_paper
max_layer: 01_canonical
next_action: "needs 09_everyday version written"
composition:
  education: 70%
  information-theory: 20%
  economics: 10%
laws_referenced: [5, 6]
status: working | canonical | published
route_class: full_framework_claim | article | everyday | evidence | toolkit | canonical_control
---
```

- **entry_layer:** where the file naturally sits NOW
- **max_layer:** how far UP it could go with work
- **next_action:** the single next thing that moves it forward

The stages below entry_layer are the TODO list.
The file's job isn't done until everything below it is populated.

Move truth downward toward the everyday person without severing 
the link back to rigor.

7. Every paper has objections listed separately from the paper body.
8. Every public article has a "what this means Monday morning" block.
9. Every page shows status: raw, developing, supported, canonical, partial, fulfilled.
10. Every domain has an index/control page.
11. Domain index pages show which stages are populated and which are TODO.
12. Empty upper layers labeled "not needed yet" or "not elevated."
13. Empty lower layers labeled "unfinished descent."
14. Articles link upward to rigor without forcing readers into it first.
15. Articles link downward to everyday/audience versions.
16. Site supports route classes: framework claim, article, everyday/ministry, evidence pack, toolkit, canonical control.
17. Evidence pages distinguish source notes from conclusions.
18. Worldcheck pages record outside-source/LLM/wiki pressure tests.
19. Fulfilled pages track what held, failed, partially held, needs revision.

## Reading Level System (from Kimi)

Every content block supports three rendering levels:

| Level | Audience | Language |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Everyday | General public, high school | No jargon, analogies, "so what?" |
| Article | College-educated, curious public | Accessible but substantive |
| Formal | PhD, specialist, reviewer | Full formalism, citations, derivations |

Same structural truth at every level. Only the language changes.
The physicist can verify the everyday version is structurally true.
The everyday reader can verify the formal version maps to what they understood.

## Cathedral Shell (from Kimi)

The website uses a 5-column layout:
- Top Row 1: Domain, Reading Level, AI Status, Session Time, Credits
- Top Row 2: Knowledge Graph, Active Claims, Confidence, Sources, Export
- Left Column: Framework context blocks (slide in based on user interaction)
- Center: Content at selected layer and reading level (or AI conversation)
- Right Column: Evidence, related papers, counter-arguments
- Bottom Bar: Lean Forward, Simplify, Deep Dive, Cite Sources, Export

The AI in the center drives block injection on the sides.
Prerendered HTML blocks slide in/out as the user explores.
The website breathes with the conversation.
