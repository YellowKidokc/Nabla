# Ascendant / Descendant / Meeting

Ascendant and Descendant use the same visual grammar. Direction and epistemic
function change; the atom shell stays the same.

## Ascendant

Evidence to Claim to Structure.

The cold-read rule applies: no Descent answer is supplied to this path. Edges
record relation type, strength, coverage, method, origin, confidence, and
assumptions.

## Descendant

Reference to Prediction to Test.

The declared reference or framework predicts what should appear before the
paper is inspected for it. Negative guards state what the descent does not
establish.

## Meeting

The Meeting projection compares Ascendant and Descendant at the same local
claim or invariant object.

- Descent yes and Ascent yes: converged
- Descent yes and Ascent no: predicted / not observed
- Descent no and Ascent yes: pressure / discovery
- Descent predicts X and Ascent finds not-X with admitted warrant: contradicted
- Both partial: unresolved or partially converged

Projection records live in `_atlas/projections.jsonl` and render into claim
HTML under the Living Atlas section.
