# Scripture Passages — Master Equation Domain

## The Trilemma in Scripture

### Justice
- **Romans 6:23a** — "For the wages of sin is death"
  The debt is real. D > 0. Someone pays.
- **Ezekiel 18:20** — "The soul who sins shall die"
  α applies to the offender. Not the class. Not the descendants.
- **Nahum 1:3** — "The LORD will by no means clear the guilty"
  J cannot be bypassed. The ledger does not erase itself.

### Mercy
- **Romans 6:23b** — "but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus"
  T_ext = D. The external cost-bearer absorbs the full amount.
- **Micah 6:8** — "to do justice, love mercy, and walk humbly"
  All three demanded simultaneously. Not as compromise — as completeness.
- **Lamentations 3:22-23** — "His mercies never come to an end"
  M does not deplete. The external source has infinite capacity.

### Free Will
- **Deuteronomy 30:19** — "I have set before you life and death... choose life"
  W = 1 requires genuine choice. Coerced virtue is not virtue.
- **Joshua 24:15** — "Choose this day whom you will serve"
  The system does not force the outcome. It presents the option.
- **Revelation 3:20** — "Behold, I stand at the door and knock"
  The cost-bearer does not break in. He waits for voluntary response.

### The Impossibility Resolved
- **Isaiah 53:5** — "He was pierced for our transgressions, crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was on him, and by his wounds we are healed"
  T_A = D (victim healed — justice). T_B = 0 (offender's debt absorbed — mercy). W_ext = 1 (voluntary — "no one takes my life from me, I lay it down of my own accord" John 10:18). The unique solution.

- **2 Corinthians 5:21** — "God made him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God"
  External to the ledger. No position in the offense. Full capacity to absorb. Not destroyed by the absorption (Resurrection).
