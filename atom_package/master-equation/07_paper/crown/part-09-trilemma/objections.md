# Part 09 — Steelmanned Objections

## Source status

The Trilemma source chapter was absent, so objections are listed as required categories rather than responses to extracted claims.

## Required objection classes

1. **Definition objection:** key terms are ambiguous or equivocated.
2. **Premise objection:** one or more premises are false or under-supported.
3. **Validity objection:** the conclusion does not follow from the premises.
4. **Scope objection:** the theorem may hold only under narrower conditions.
5. **Countermodel objection:** a coherent model may satisfy the premises while denying the conclusion.
