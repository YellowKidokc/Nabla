# Equation Completeness and Universal Atom Layer

Status: draft proposal, not canon promotion.

Purpose:

Capture the next step in the General Theory scaffold: the project cannot stop at ten laws. It needs an extensible atom layer for all of physics, an extensible atom layer for spiritual terms, and a disciplined standard for deciding when a claimed equation or bridge is complete enough to work.

## Core Recognition

The ten laws are not the whole project.

They are a spine.

Physics contains many more concepts than the ten selected law families: state, force, energy, field, boundary, phase, symmetry, resonance, diffusion, scattering, optics, stress, pressure, potential, entropy, equilibrium, information, measurement, noise, and more.

Spiritual language is also larger than ten headline terms: grace, sin, peace, love, mercy, judgment, faith, patience, repentance, truth, deception, holiness, wisdom, obedience, worship, forgiveness, reconciliation, sanctification, and more.

The atom system should eventually hold both vocabularies.

The bridge layer should show where they correspond, where they merely resemble, and where they do not map.

## The Equation Corset

Use this as the completeness standard for any equation-like claim.

An equation is not just symbols on a line. A full equation atom must have the necessary ingredients that let it define something, do work, and be checked.

### Required Ingredients

| Ingredient | Question It Answers | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| `phenomenon` | What is this equation about? | Prevents free-floating math |
| `quantity_defined` | What does the equation define or compute? | Tells the reader what output means |
| `variables` | What symbols appear? | Prevents hidden meanings |
| `domains` | What values may each variable take? | Prevents impossible inputs |
| `units_dimensions` | What are the units or dimensions? | Guards physical nonsense |
| `operators` | What operations transform the variables? | Shows how the equation works |
| `parameters_constants` | What fixed values or tunable values appear? | Separates universal from fitted |
| `state_space` | What kind of system-state does it act on? | Links equation to atoms |
| `boundary_conditions` | What is held fixed or crossed? | Defines the model edges |
| `initial_conditions` | Where does the process begin? | Makes prediction possible |
| `regime` | Where is this equation valid? | Stops overextension |
| `assumptions` | What must be true for it to apply? | Makes the premise audit visible |
| `invariants` | What remains unchanged? | Connects to conservation/symmetry |
| `failure_modes` | When does it break? | Makes falsification possible |
| `interpretation` | What does the result mean? | Prevents symbol worship |
| `measurement_protocol` | How could it be observed or estimated? | Connects math to reality |
| `source_receipts` | Where did it come from? | Keeps provenance honest |
| `negative_guards` | What must not be inferred? | Stops overclaiming |
| `kill_conditions` | What would demote or falsify it? | Keeps the claim scientific |

## Equation Status Types

Not every equation-like thing has the same status.

Use these statuses:

| Status | Meaning | Allowed Claim |
|---|---|---|
| `definition_equation` | Defines a quantity or relation | "This defines X." |
| `descriptive_equation` | Describes observed relation | "This models X in this regime." |
| `dynamical_equation` | Evolves a state through time/process | "This governs change under assumptions." |
| `constraint_equation` | Restricts possible states | "Only states satisfying this are allowed." |
| `conservation_equation` | Tracks invariant/accounting | "This quantity is conserved under conditions." |
| `bridge_equation_candidate` | Proposed cross-domain mapping | "This is a candidate bridge." |
| `formal_model_equation` | Works inside a formal model | "Inside these premises, this follows." |
| `empirical_test_equation` | Predicts a measurable result | "This can be tested by this protocol." |
| `metaphor_equation` | Symbolic or explanatory only | "This illustrates; it does not prove." |

## Process Completeness

Some bridges are not equations yet. They are processes.

A process atom needs:

- input state
- output state
- transformation
- actor or mechanism
- boundary crossed
- cost or conservation account
- time/order
- possible failure modes
- observable manifestation
- relation to Master Equation

Examples:

- sin -> disorder increase
- grace -> restoration / negentropic correction
- repentance -> state transition
- forgiveness -> relational accounting transformation
- sanctification -> iterative coherence increase
- deception -> signal distortion
- discernment -> noise filtering / truth recognition

## Mapping Completeness

A bridge from physics to spiritual meaning is complete enough for serious review only when it can answer:

1. What is the physical atom?
2. What is the spiritual atom?
3. What properties are being mapped?
4. What properties are not being mapped?
5. What direction does the mapping run?
6. Is the mapping reversible?
7. What is preserved?
8. What is reflected?
9. What breaks the mapping?
10. What countermodel has been tried?
11. What bridge grade is justified?
12. What Master Equation role does it support?

Until these are answered, the bridge remains analogy or candidate correspondence.

## How This Links To The Master Equation

The Master Equation is the central coherence model.

But the Master Equation needs the full physics/spiritual atom layers because each factor depends on many support concepts.

For example:

- `G` external negentropy influx needs flux, boundary, source, open system, conservation guard, grace, gift, and active giving atoms.
- `E` signal propagation fidelity needs signal, noise, medium, distortion, truth, deception, witness, and testimony atoms.
- `S_eff` effective entropy factor needs entropy, disorder, random motion, decay, sin, corruption, and repair atoms.
- `T` temporal integration needs time, duration, memory, patience, persistence, and sanctification atoms.
- `K` information compression ratio needs compression, meaning, simplicity, Logos, parable, and deception-cost atoms.
- `R` phase transition indicator needs threshold, state change, repentance, conversion, and new creation atoms.
- `Q` superposition measure needs potential, unresolved state, choice, faith, actualization, and agency atoms.
- `F` non-local correlation strength needs relation, entanglement language, communion, prayer, and intercession atoms.
- `C` total integration measure needs coherence, unity, Christ, body, reconciliation, and wholeness atoms.

So the map is:

```text
physical atom -> spiritual atom -> bridge/process atom -> Master Equation role -> paper/mind-map output
```

## What Shows We Are Moving In The Right Direction

This scaffold is one more step because it does not ask the reader to accept the final claim first.

It builds the necessary conditions:

1. List the physical vocabulary thoroughly.
2. List the spiritual vocabulary thoroughly.
3. Define each atom separately.
4. Define the bridge separately.
5. Grade the bridge honestly.
6. Attach the bridge to the Master Equation only after its role is clear.
7. Require equation/process completeness before stronger claims.

That is the direction of a real system rather than a pile of analogies.

- state
