# Theophysics Canon — A/B/C Compilation

**Start here:** open `index.html` in any browser.

This folder is a first-pass compilation of the Theophysics Canon using an A/B/C register grid. It is intended for internal review and as a staging area before a future full rewrite.

## Reading rules

- **A — Theology:** declarative Christian claims (God, Trinity, creation, fall, redemption, grace, etc.). Theology leads.
- **B — Science:** empirical and formal claims, bounded to what physics, information theory, logic, and mathematics can actually show. Science witnesses; it does not prove doctrine.
- **C — Theophysics:** the bridge register. It names structural rhymes, candidate derivations, and correspondence maps. Bridges are labeled as partial, pending, retired, or gap.

Missing perspectives are marked explicitly (`O3A`, `O3B`, `O3C`) instead of being invented.

## Layout

- `index.html` — the backgrid: every topic with its A/B/C status.
- `topics/abc-*.html` — one page per topic, with A/B/C sections.
- `assets/abc-style.css` — shared styles and responsive grid.
- `assets/abc-nav.js` — filter buttons on the backgrid.
- `abc-map.json` — machine-readable topic/status map.
- Numbered folders (`00 - ...`, `01 - ...`, etc.) — source archives copied from `theophysics-canon/`, `blue-pages/`, and other working folders. Their internal relative links are preserved from the originals and may not resolve inside this compilation folder.

## Status badges

- **Present** — content exists and is in register.
- **Partial** — content exists but is explicitly bounded or incomplete.
- **Gap** — no claim is made in this register; the missing perspective is named.
- **Locked** — at least one perspective in the row has a strong or verified status.

## Honesty boundaries

- No silent upgrades: science is not claimed to prove God, Christianity, the Master Equation, moral realism, or a self-grounding substrate.
- No flattened Christianity: Jesus, the Trinity, sin, grace, and atonement are not reduced to metaphor only.
- Gaps, kill conditions, and owed derivations are displayed openly.

## Known limitations

- This is an aggregation map, not the final rewrite.
- Some copied source-archive HTMLs contain relative links to sibling files that were not copied here.
- The spiritual-terms dictionary is shared from `../theophysics-canon/07-spiritual-terms.html`.
