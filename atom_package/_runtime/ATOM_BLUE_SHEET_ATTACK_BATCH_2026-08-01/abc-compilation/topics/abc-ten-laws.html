Ten Laws

The law walk maps physical structures to spiritual meanings. Every law keeps its derivation grade visible; retired claims stay retired.

A - Theology

Spiritual Structures

The laws are read as structures of truth, judgment, mercy, faith, atonement, and redemption. They are not ten commandments. They are ten structural mirrors: physical laws whose equilibrium states carry spiritual terms that emerge, not imposed from outside.

B - Science

Named Physics Sources

The law walk draws from gravitation, mechanics, electromagnetism, the strong force, thermodynamics, information theory, quantum mechanics, the weak force, and relativity. Each physics source must stand on its own terms. The Master Equation wraps them; it does not replace GR, QCD, Maxwell, quantum mechanics, or thermodynamics.

Three Laws Worth Highlighting

Law V — Thermodynamics / Judgment. The closest thing to a clean derivation. The Second Law already says closed systems cannot rescue themselves. The R(offense, α) function holds justice and mercy simultaneously: α = 1 means the offender pays; α = 0 means a third party pays; α = 0 plus judge-as-payer is the Cross. The spiritual reading does not need to add a free-will switch to make the structure interesting.

Law VIII — Weak Force / Moral Conservation. The weak interaction transmutes, violates parity, and keeps the books. Modeled as a three-body event: ψ_whole → ψ_broken + δ + ν_loss. The result is simple and brutal: you cannot undo it, and it does not go away. Suppression is not repair.

Law IV — Strong Force / Love. Protons that should repel hold together because a close-range binding is stronger. Agape is covenantal binding under stress. The "all nine fruits from one Hamiltonian" claim is retired; the honest structure is Tier 1 (Love → Peace → Joy) plus Tier 2 lenses.

  • Landauer proves grace.

Pair Structure

Four pairs, one herald, one wrapper:

  • P1: Newton–Grace / Einstein–Frame — pending AM-1 (restore IX or rule eight laws + wrapper).
  • P2: Newton–Momentum / Fermi–Conservation — strengthened.
  • P3: Yukawa–Agape / Heisenberg–Faith — holds.
  • P4: Clausius–Judgment / Shannon–Logos — strongest.
  • Herald: Maxwell–Truth — light announces the wrapper rather than pairing symmetrically.
  • Wrapper: Coherence–Christ — closure, not peer mechanism.

Honesty Boundary

The grades are not modesty theater. Law V and Law VIII are genuinely stronger because less had to be added. Laws I–IV, VI, and VII are partial or conjectural. Law IX is pending a ruling. The wrapper is definitional. The framework does not claim every law is equally proven; it claims the structure is honest enough to show where the work remains.
