A/B/C Topic

Cost and Shadow Doorway

The shadow-before-creation and cost pages are strong doorway material. Cost needs review before broad sharing.

A - Theology

Shadow, Justice, Mercy, Cost

Theology frames the possibility of evil, the reality of sin, and the convergence of justice, mercy, and cost at the Cross. The clean line: evil is not a created substance. Evil is the away-direction made possible by the reality of the Good, and made enterable only when finite freedom exists.

The shadow was cast at creation as bounded possibility. It was entered at the Fall as actual refusal. Creation carries a structural cost; creatures carry a moral responsibility. The two are separate, and both are real.

B - Science

Cost Without Moralizing

Physics can describe entropy, irreversibility, conservation, and cost. It cannot by itself say that a moral debt has been paid or that grace exists. The framework distinguishes structural entropy (the neutral cost of finitude) from moral entropy (the directional fracture of will). Confusing them either makes God the author of evil or excuses human cruelty.

C - Theophysics

Strong, But Review Needed

The shadow page is strong and mostly family-safe. The cost page is marked "do not send yet" in the audit because it may predate later caveats or retired claims.

Locked bridge claims: structural entropy ≠ moral entropy; "very good" includes the cost of finitude; the Fall is a relational breach (removal of maintenance), not a physics change.

Open: "Tree of Life as grace-maintenance" is interpretive, not derived from axioms. The cost page's claims should be cross-checked against the latest canon rulings before broad sharing.
