A/B/C Topic

Hard Questions and Objections

The canon must face the hardest questions directly and keep objections visible instead of hiding them under confidence.

A - Theology

Hard Texts Stay In

Theology cannot protect itself by deleting scandal, judgment, or difficult biblical texts. The posture is reverent honesty, not moral authority stolen from God. The Bible itself models honest questioning: Job defends his integrity to God's face; Habakkuk demands God account for injustice; Jesus cries, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?"

The Job posture — questioning in loyalty — is the only one that preserves both truth and love. It refuses the four dangerous postures: closed book (atheism), apologist (conscience silenced), authority (God as tyrant), and adversarial (taking God's seat).

B - Science

Physics Says Nothing Here

Physics has no category for divine command, moral horror, or theodicy. Anyone who tries to solve the conquest narratives with entropy or information theory is doing something other than physics. The hard-questions page is not a physics page.

C - Theophysics

Objection Posture And Lean Guardrail

Theophysics should invite real objections, mark where they land, and either answer, downgrade, quarantine, or retire claims. That process is part of the truth claim.

The framework provides a structure for holding hard passages honestly without collapsing into apologetics, atheism, or authoritarianism. The Split format is a guardrail, not a solution. It prevents the four postures from collapsing into each other.

Lean gold ticket: T-01 formalizes the Job posture under defined predicates. It does not prove historical interpretation or resolve every hard passage.
