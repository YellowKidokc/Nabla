A/B/C Topic

Science Triads

A source map for three-role structures in physics, information theory, and measurement. Counting threes is cheap; functional irreducibility is the criterion.

A - Theology

Creation May Bear Traces

Theology may expect creation to bear structural traces of its Creator. That expectation does not become a physics premise. It belongs in theology and bridge. The claim is not that every group of three proves the Trinity; it is that stable created order may reflect the relational grammar of its source.

B - Science

Actual Scientific Structures

A real triad requires irreducible roles, functional dependence, mediation, asymmetry, and formal visibility. Examples that meet this standard:

  • Shannon communication: source, channel, receiver.
  • Quantum measurement: system, measurement context, outcome.
  • Decoherence / einselection: system, environment, pointer states.
  • Open quantum systems: system, bath, coupling term.
  • Noether's theorem: action / Lagrangian, symmetry transformation, conserved quantity.
  • Error correction: encoded data, syndrome/check, recovery operation.

Each must be described in its own scientific terms first. The third role is not decorative; it mediates, stabilizes, selects, preserves, or makes the relation public.

C - Theophysics

Not Proof By Counting

Theophysics does not say every group of three proves the Trinity. It asks whether stable truth repeatedly needs being, mediation, and record; source, channel, and receiver; system, context, and outcome.

Core pattern: state + mediation + stable witness. The bridge claim is that this recurring grammar is structurally convergent with the Trinity's relational logic. The claim is [C]-grade correspondence, not proof.

Best bridges to use first: decoherence, Shannon communication, Noether, open quantum systems, thermodynamics (system/boundary/environment), error correction, gauge theory.

Avoid as primary: CPT as direct Trinity proof, holography as proof of Logos, Higgs as grace, Casimir as common grace. They may be beautiful later; they are not first load-bearing cases.
