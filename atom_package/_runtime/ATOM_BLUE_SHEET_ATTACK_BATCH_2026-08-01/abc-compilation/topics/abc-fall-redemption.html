A/B/C Topic

Fall and Redemption

Theological origin of evil, science silence before physical systems, and Theophysics bridge models for closure and repair.

A - Theology

Evil As Refusal, Not Substance

Evil is not a thing God created beside the Good. It is the away-direction made possible by real reception and refusal. Grace is not plan B; it is present in the purpose of God before history unfolds. The Descent walks from "God creates" to "Christ is the convergence point" in 24 steps: every major bridge is named so it can be attacked.

The key hinge is step 8: relation reveals value. Once something can be honored or violated, orientation becomes real. Agency, finitude, decay, and adversarial refusal follow.

B - Science

O3B - Science Gap

Science can describe physical systems, entropy, noise, boundaries, and openness. It has no register for moral origin before physical systems exist. This gap should be marked, not filled with invented science.

C - Theophysics

Closure And Repair

Theophysics can model refusal as closure, loss of coherence, and increasing drift. It can model grace as reopening from beyond a closed system. These are bridge models, not physics proofs of the Fall.

The central formal results live in Law V and Law VIII. R(offense, α) holds justice and mercy simultaneously: α = 1 (offender pays), α = 0 (third party pays), and α = 0 + judge-as-payer is the Cross — the unique point where both justice and mercy reach their maximum. Law VIII models moral breakdown as a three-body decay: ψ_whole → ψ_broken + δ + ν_loss. The ledger does not forget; grace does not pretend.
