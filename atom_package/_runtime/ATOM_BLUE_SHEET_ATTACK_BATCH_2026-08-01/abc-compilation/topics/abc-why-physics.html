A/B/C Topic

Why God Made Physics

Physics is not God. Physics is the ordered created medium where relation, consequence, choice, time, repair, embodiment, and love can become publicly meaningful.

A - Theology

Creation As Gift

God does not need physics. Creatures need a lawful, finite, temporal world in which love can become action, choice can carry cost, and grace can arrive as rescue. The world is the stage where love is no longer private intention.

A meal, a wound, a hand extended, a body broken, and a tomb found empty all require embodied consequence. The Incarnation is the ultimate L1–L4 bridge: the Creator enters creation, the infinite becomes finite, the sustainer becomes the sustained.

B - Science

Lawful Consequence

Physics describes intelligibility, stable laws, distinguishable states, irreversible processes, time, embodiment, and open systems. It does not assign divine purpose to those features. The question "why is there a lawful world?" is not answered by any equation inside physics.

C - Theophysics

The Missing Middle

The bridge says physics is the stage where love is no longer private intention. Embodiment, cost, time, and consequence make relation public and testable. The same laws that permit freedom also enforce cost: a world where choices matter requires a world where choices have effects.

Honesty boundary: this is a theological/bridge interpretation of why a lawful created order is fitting, not a physics proof that God had to create physics. The "why" belongs to A and C; B describes the "what."
