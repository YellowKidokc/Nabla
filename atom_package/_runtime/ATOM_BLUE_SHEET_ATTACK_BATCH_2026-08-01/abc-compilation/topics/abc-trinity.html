A/B/C Topic

Truth and Trinity

The Trinity is stated theologically first. Scientific triads are treated as witnesses or correspondences, not proofs.

A - Theology

One God, Three Persons

Christian theology confesses Father, Son, and Spirit: source/being, Word/expression, and witness/communion. The claim is doctrinal and theological before it is a bridge claim. The persons are their relations: the Father is the one who begets, the Son is the one begotten, the Spirit is the one who proceeds. Remove the relation and you remove the person.

The four axioms map onto this ground: existence (Father), distinction / Word (Son), relation (Spirit), orientation / Truth (voiced by the Son). The Church Fathers called this perichoresis — mutual indwelling — and the medievals called it the transcendentals: being, one, true, good, one reality read four ways.

B - Science

Triadic Roles In Explanation

Science contains repeated three-role structures: system / context / outcome; source / channel / receiver; system / environment / pointer record. These are real structures in the science register. Physics does not need the Trinity to calculate, and triadic structure does not prove the Trinity.

The strongest science-side anchors include Noether's theorem (law, symmetry transformation, conserved quantity), decoherence (system, environment, pointer states), and Shannon communication (source, channel, receiver). Each is load-bearing inside physics; each becomes a bridge candidate only after its scientific content is stated in its own terms.

C - Theophysics

Correspondence Grade — And Five Instances

Theophysics asks whether recurring triads are accidental, emergent, brute, or grounded. The safe claim is structural convergence. The forbidden claim is "there are threes, therefore Christianity is proven."
