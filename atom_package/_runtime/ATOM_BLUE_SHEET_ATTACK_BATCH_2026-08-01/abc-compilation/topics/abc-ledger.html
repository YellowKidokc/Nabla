A/B/C Topic

Ledger and Proof Status

The ledger is the project's conscience: what survived, what was repaired, what is retired, and what remains owed.

A - Theology

Truthfulness In The Open

If God is Truth, the framework must tell the truth about itself. Corrections are not shameful; they are part of fidelity to truth. An argument that hides its attackers isn't an argument.

B - Science

Claim Types Must Stay Separate

Proof, model, analogy, correspondence, conjecture, and open question are different grades. The science register gets damaged when they are mixed. The audit protocol is: apparent objection → corpus search → structural test → disposition: killed, reduced, open, repaired, derivation-owed, or strengthened.

C - Theophysics

Governance Layer

The ledger, Lean gold ticket standard, and Lane 4 registry govern canon status. A bridge can pass with caveats. A failed claim can retire without collapsing the whole project.

What walked through untouched: the triadic termination structure, the coupling gate battery, and the method itself — state the strongest objection, search for an independent repair, attack again, keep only what survives.
