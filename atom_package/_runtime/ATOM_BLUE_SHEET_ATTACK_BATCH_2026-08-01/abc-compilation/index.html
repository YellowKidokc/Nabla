Theophysics Canon - A/B/C Compilation

Backgrid

Every major topic gets three registers: A - Theology, B - Science, and C - Theophysics bridge. Missing perspectives are marked as gaps instead of being invented.

Reading rule: Theology leads, science witnesses, bridges are labeled. A claim must be judged in the register where it belongs. This is an aggregation map, not the final rewrite.
