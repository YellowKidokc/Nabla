The Rules of This Track

What this series is, and what it refuses to be

This is the parallel spine. Every sheet numbered with an A runs on axioms, mathematics, and physics alone. The discipline is total and checkable:

1

No theological vocabulary. Not as metaphor, not as color, not once. If a word needs a church to define it, it does not appear in an A-sheet.

2

Every citation is to philosophy, mathematics, or physics. Nothing else is load-bearing.

3

Every conclusion must survive a reader who rejects every religious claim ever made. That reader is the intended audience.

4

Any smuggled premise found in an A-sheet is a defect. Report it; it gets cut in the open.

If you believe nothing beyond what can be argued, this track is yours. Take it on. See if you can beat it.

The Axioms

Four assumptions. Stated, not hidden.

Every formal system pays its way in axioms. ZFC pays nine. Peano pays five. General relativity pays two postulates; quantum mechanics pays five. Nobody calls those systems theology. This track pays four — and unlike most systems, it itemizes the bill on page one.

◆

A1 — Existence POSITED

Something exists. The one assumption no argument can precede, since arguments exist. Why there is something rather than nothing — "for nothing is simpler and easier than something" Leibniz, Principles of Nature and Grace §7, 1714 — is a question this track does not answer. It takes the something as given.

A2 — Distinction POSITED

Difference is real. This is not that; a thing is not its negation. Aristotle, Metaphysics IV From real difference, information is possible: a distinction that can be registered is a bit. Shannon, A Mathematical Theory of Communication, 1948 Without A2 there is no measurement, no thought, no this sentence.

A3 — Relation POSITED

Distinguishable things interact lawfully. Regularly, repeatably, everywhere tested. Induction cannot ground itself — no experiment proves that experiments generalize Hume, Enquiry IV, 1748 — so lawfulness is taken as an axiom, exactly as every laboratory on Earth silently takes it.

Split A3.1 — Relation and relational ontology. A3 posits relation and lawful interaction. The stronger claim that relation is primitive, not derived from space or time, is a philosophical bridge claim, not a settled physics result. Physics can be read relationally, and general relativity gives that reading real weight, but this A-sheet marks the upgrade openly instead of hiding it.

A4 — Orientation POSITED

Some states rank above others on at least one real gradient: signal above noise, truth above error, coherence above dissolution. No "ought" follows from any pile of "is" Hume, Treatise III.i.1, 1739 — so the gradient cannot be derived and is posited instead. Note what denying it costs: the denial "this axiom is false" claims truth beats error, which is the gradient in use. A4 may be held as objective value or as the minimal ranking any inquiry presupposes; the track runs on either reading.

Four axioms. Itemized, priced, paid in the open. Everything after this line is derivation, and every derivation can be checked.
From Axioms To Created Structure

The minimal ontology, then the instantiations

The floor of this track is not space, matter, motion, and time as four independent primitives. The colder floor is existence, distinction, relation, and orientation. From there, the created structures can be read as instantiations: information as distinguished content in relation, order as directed relation, space as a domain of co-existent relations, time as sequential relation, matter as instantiated physical content, and motion as ordered change.

Bridge grade. This is valid bridge language, not a finished physics derivation. The symbols K, G, Ω, and T belong to the Master Equation grammar. Their physical instantiation still requires the relevant physics-register machinery: the Standard Model, general relativity, equations of motion, conservation laws, and symmetry groups.
What This Track Does Not Do

The debts stay unpaid — on purpose

The companion series asks why these four cohere and offers an answer. This track does not ask. Here the four are brute: unexplained, unapologized for, exactly as brute as spacetime's postulates or set theory's nine. The refusal is a feature. It means every result downstream belongs to anyone who accepts the four — which, as the axiom statements show, is anyone who has ever measured, argued, or called a claim false.

Where the Track Goes

The road, axioms only

01A

The single expression: on the relational reading, space, matter, motion, and time admit no clean ordering — each is constituted through the others. Relational space against the absolute container Leibniz–Clarke Correspondence, 1715–16; geometry and content co-defining in general relativity Einstein 1915; Wheeler; time as the number of motion Aristotle, Physics IV.11. One package, no first member inside this model.

02A

The grammar: any system maintaining structure against noise needs an ordering rate, an information density, and a possibility space — a coherence functional as schema, with its open formalization problems stated, not hidden. K is meaningful information density in the grammar; its instantiation as matter-energy requires the physics register.

03A

The cost: information is physical; ordering has a price Landauer 1961; Shannon 1948. Structure far from equilibrium persists only by exporting entropy Prigogine, dissipative structures, Nobel 1977. The tension that builds also dissolves — and that tension is where complexity lives.

04A

The receiver: systems that register distinctions and act on gradients — observation, agency, and the ranking A4 already priced in.

The Challenge

Beat it, or absorb it

The claim of this track is narrow and testable: from these four axioms, the chain that follows is valid — each step forced, no step smuggled. To defeat it, do one of two things: show a fifth hidden axiom doing secret work, or break a derivation step. Either kill is accepted publicly and the sheet gets amended in the open.

And if the chain holds? Then its conclusions stand on your floor, whoever you are — because you already paid these four axioms the moment you started checking. What remains, for anyone who wants it, is a further question this track is forbidden to ask: four brute assumptions, or one ground that pays for all four and explains why they cohere. A companion series takes that question up. This one never needs it.

Four axioms. One chain.
Break it if you can.
