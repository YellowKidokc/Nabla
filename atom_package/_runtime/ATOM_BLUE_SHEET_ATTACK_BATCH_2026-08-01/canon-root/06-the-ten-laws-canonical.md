## Law Walk

INewton-Grace - Gravitation / ConvergenceMapped

Why God Built ThisGod knew His children would wander. A wandering system needs a pull that does not quit, a field that reaches across distance, a curve that holds even when the held thing does not understand it. So creation gives you gravity before it gives you the word grace. Every step is taken inside a holding you did not generate. Physical strangeness

Gravity is not mainly a push or a shove. In the relativistic picture, mass-energy curves spacetime and bodies move along the curve. The apple falls because the shape of the world tells it how to move. The orbit is not self-powered striving. It is free fall.

Central-field candidate: Phi_g(r) = -mu_g / r, a(r) = -grad Phi_g(r) = -mu_g r_hat / r^2 Spiritual law

Grace-as-geometry is the given curvature of created reality toward God. Sin is departure from that geodesic. Under the July 29 ruling, `R` is proper acceleration: deviation from free fall. Refusal is not stillness. Refusal is thrust away from the path that would have carried you.

X_G includes refusal asymmetry: 1 - R(t)/R_max Interpretation

This law says grace is not first a felt intervention. It is the prior shape of the world. You can resist it, but resistance is not a competing field. It is non-geodesic motion. The spiritual surprise is severe: rebellion and works-righteousness become the same category of motion when both are self-thrust. Only free fall is grace.

#### Record / open repairs

- The old two-body expression is reciprocal and cannot be the main Creator-to-creature model. AM-3 recommends the central-field repair.
- The two-body form may survive as creature-to-creature relational dynamics, not the God-to-soul direction.
- Kill condition: if a system reaches the source by self-generated thrust, the grace-as-geodesic reading fails.

IINewton-Momentum - Mechanics of ResponseMapped Upgraded

Why God Built ThisA soul with momentum does not redirect itself by wishing. Habit has inertia. Shame has inertia. Addiction has inertia. God built mechanics so you could feel the difference between wanting a different path and actually receiving the impulse that turns you. Physical strangeness

Momentum persists. A body keeps moving unless an external impulse changes its state. Force is not vague motivation; it integrates over time into a real change of momentum.

p = m v; F = dp/dt; J = integral F dt = Delta p Spiritual law

Sin nature is moral inertia: the resistance of a person to change of state. Repentance is not self-generated redirection. It is response to grace-as-impulse. Law I carries grace as the unfelt geometry of free fall; Law II carries grace as the felt impulse that turns an entrenched trajectory onto that geodesic.

Grace-as-impulse: J_grace = integral F_ext dt -> Delta p_soul Interpretation

The turn is felt because it is acceleration relative to the old path. But the impulse is not meant to continue forever. Once the trajectory is turned, the thrust ends and free fall resumes. That is the reconciliation between Law I and Law II: grace turns, then grace carries.

#### Record / open repairs

- Mass-energy is no longer the eponym. It is pending David as interior vs commentary. AM-7 recommends commentary for the present revision.
- The old `gamma(R) = (1 - R^2/c^2)^(-1/2)` is dimensionally invalid if R is acceleration. Repair required: use a dimensionless acceleration ratio, velocity gamma separately, or rapidity from integrated proper acceleration.
- Lean target: equal impulse gives equal momentum change regardless of dramatic peak. This protects undramatic conversion.

Why God Built ThisThis is the hardest lesson and the most merciful one. A closed system runs down. God built decay into the classroom so nobody could honestly believe self-enclosure is life. Apart from the source, everything we preserve eventually disperses. Physical strangeness

The Second Law does not say disorder usually wins. It says entropy in a closed system does not decrease. The law is not cruelty. It is bookkeeping. Heat, cost, work, and irreversibility all have to be paid somewhere.

dS/dt >= 0 for a closed system; F = E - T S Spiritual law

Judgment is truthful thermodynamic accounting in the moral domain. Mercy is not the cancellation of cost. Mercy is the Judge becoming payer without making the ledger lie.

R(offense, alpha): alpha=1 -> offender pays; alpha=0 -> third party pays; alpha=0 + judge=payer -> Cross Interpretation

This is the closest thing to derivation in the set because nothing had to be added. The law already says closed systems cannot rescue themselves. The spiritual reading does not need a free-will switch to make the structure interesting.
