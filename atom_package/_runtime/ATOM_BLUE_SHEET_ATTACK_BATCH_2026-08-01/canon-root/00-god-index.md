## Reader Rule

This canon uses the Cross-Resolution Method.

Every major claim must be read in the register where it belongs:

- **Theology:** what Christian doctrine, Scripture, and confession say.
- **Physics:** what established models, equations, and experiments permit or forbid by themselves.
- **Mathematics:** what follows from definitions and proofs.
- **Lived reality:** what people actually recognize in conscience, grief, love, guilt, beauty, and hope.
- **Bridge:** where the same structure appears across registers as analogy, resonance, model, theorem, convergence, or open conjecture.

Physics is not allowed to smuggle in theology. Theology is not allowed to rescue weak physics. Story is not allowed to pretend it is proof. Formal proof is not allowed to pretend it has captured the whole meaning of lived experience.

But when the same structure appears across registers, we do not dismiss it merely because it crosses a boundary. We mark the bridge, name its strength, state its limits, and keep testing.

No hidden upgrades.
