Part 0 — The Story

## Before anything is assumed, Someone is

Every person, at some point, looks up and wonders: _Why is there something rather than nothing?_ It's not a question for philosophers alone. A child asks it staring at stars. A parent asks it holding a newborn. A soldier asks it in a foxhole. The question is older than any answer, and every answer starts by assuming the very thing it tries to explain.

This document starts one step earlier. Not with an assumption. With a Person.

Before there was time, before there was space, before there was matter or motion or law — there was love. Not love as a feeling. Love as a relationship. A Father, a Son, and the Spirit between them — one being, three persons, complete in themselves, needing nothing outside themselves. The only thing that has ever been self-sufficient. The only ground that doesn't need a ground beneath it.

Everything else — every star, every atom, every law, every heartbeat — flows from this. Not because the Trinity was lonely. Because love, by its nature, overflows. Because a fountain that is full must spill.

And God said unto Moses, I AM THAT I AM.Exodus 3:14

You don't need a degree to understand this. You need a memory. Remember the last time you felt truly loved — not for what you did, but for who you were. That is a fingerprint of the Trinity. Love without performance. Presence without condition. Relationship without need.

Four assumptions. Each true. Each unable to pay for itself. Every rival ontology dies or collapses precisely because it stands on these four with no receipt.
