# Derivation Grammar v0.2

Unified Derivation Grammar Schema · Type-normalized candidate
POF 2828 · Formal grammar / meta-framework · Theology-free at the grammar layer · 2026-08-01

## 0\. Purpose

Unified Derivation Grammar (DG) is a formal language for testing layered derivations. It does not begin by naming God, Christ, Trinity, Ehad, Logos, physics, consciousness, morality, or any other domain-specific conclusion. It begins with a simpler question:

> What must be true for one layer of reality, explanation, or meaning to depend on another without erasing it?

DG tests: dependency, necessity, inheritance, irreducibility, collapse, translation, closure, and convergence. The grammar belongs before worldview claims. A worldview may instantiate the grammar. The grammar itself does not belong to any one of them.

## 1\. Type Normalization

v0.1 mixed objects, relations, and operators in one list. v0.2 separates them.

### Objects

Symbol| Name| Meaning
---|---|---
L| Layer| A level in a derivation staircase
C| Capability| A new irreducible operation introduced at a layer
S| State| The condition of a layer or derivation: Coherent, Defective, or Ruptured

### Relations

Relation| Meaning
---|---
DependsOn(L2, L1)| Layer L2 requires Layer L1
TranslatesTo(C1, C2)| Capability C1 is carried into another register as C2 while preserving relevant structure

### Modal Operator

Operator| Meaning
---|---
Constraint(L)| The admissibility condition governing what Layer L can say, deny, inherit, or translate

This is the key type correction. Dependency and translation are relations. Constraint is modal. Layer, capability, and state are objects.

## 2\. Core Rule

L(n+1) = L(n) + C(n+1)

A higher layer inherits the valid capabilities of the lower layer and adds one new irreducible capability class. Higher layers may contextualize, constrain, complete, or reveal the limits of lower layers. But a valid higher layer does not abolish the lower layer it depends on.

**Reductionism** says the higher is nothing but the lower.
**Segregation** says the layers do not truly touch.
**DG** says the higher depends, preserves, and adds.

## 3\. Axioms

DG1

Dependency

DependsOn(L(n+1), L(n))

Every non-root layer depends on at least one prior layer. A derived layer cannot float without prerequisites.

DG2

Minimal Capability

C(n+1) is not reducible to C(0)...C(n)

Each new layer must introduce at least one capability not already available in the prior layers. If no new capability is added, the layer is only a restatement.

DG3

Preservation / Monotonic Inheritance

Valid(L(n+1)) → Preserves(L(n+1), L(n))

The load-bearing axiom. A valid higher layer preserves the valid capabilities of the lower layer. Physics is not abolished by biology; biology is not abolished by consciousness; consciousness is not abolished by theology. The higher layer is not allowed to win by destroying the floor it stands on.

DG4

Collapse

¬L(n) → ¬L(n+1)

If a required lower layer collapses, any layer that depends on it also collapses unless an explicit repair or alternate dependency is supplied. Collapse propagates upward through dependency.

DG5

Translation

TranslatesTo(C1, C2) → StructurePreserved(C1, C2)

A capability may be translated from one register into another only if its relevant structure is preserved. Different language is allowed. Different structure is not.

DG6

Dynamics

State ∈ {Coherent, Defective, Ruptured}

Layers and derivations can occupy coherent, defective, or ruptured states. The grammar must describe both success and failure modes.

DG7

Admissibility

ValidStep(L(n), L(n+1)) iff DependsOn ∧ NewCapability ∧ Preserves

A derivation step is valid only if its dependencies exist, its new capability is genuinely new, and its prior layer is preserved. This gives the grammar its testing function.

DG8

Closure

¬(Denies(x, C) ∧ RequiresToFormulate(x, C))

A derivation cannot deny a capability whose use is required to formulate the denial. If a claim uses truth, logic, distinction, or intelligibility in order to deny all truth, logic, distinction, or intelligibility, the claim fails closure.

## 4\. Theorem Candidates

T1 — NO SKIP

The staircase cannot skip a required layer.

DependsOn(L3, L2) ∧ DependsOn(L2, L1) → Requires(L3, L2)

T2 — COLLAPSE PROPAGATION

Dependency creates vulnerability.

¬L1 ∧ DependsOn(L2, L1) → ¬L2

T3 — TRANSLATION INVARIANCE

Different language is allowed. Different structure is not.

TranslatesTo(CA, CB) → PreservesStructure(CA, CB)

T4 — INDEPENDENT CONVERGENCE

Two frameworks with equivalent structure instantiate a shared abstract derivation object.

Models(A, DG) ∧ Models(B, DG) ∧ EquivalentStructure(F(A), F(B)) → shared G

Not yet proved. The most important proposed formal criterion of v0.2. To claim Independent Convergence: both frameworks satisfy DG1–DG8 independently, preserve the same dependency graph, introduce equivalent capability classes, preserve collapse and translation behavior, and neither borrows its result from the other at the grammar level.

## 5\. The Four Questions

Every DG node should answer:

Q1 — WHAT MUST ALREADY EXIST?

Identifies dependency. What prior layer or capability does this claim require?

Q2 — WHAT NEW CAPABILITY APPEARS?

Identifies irreducibility. What can be done now that could not be done before?

Q3 — WHAT COLLAPSES IF IT IS REMOVED?

Identifies necessity. What higher layers fail if this layer is denied?

Q4 — HOW DOES IT TRANSLATE?

Identifies bridge discipline. What preserves across registers, and what does not?

## 6\. Theology-Free Zone

DG must not name theological conclusions at the grammar layer. If the grammar names the Trinity, it becomes a Christian framework testing Christianity by Christian terms. If the grammar names Ehad, it becomes an Islamic framework testing Islam by Islamic terms. If the grammar names neither, it becomes a shared formal language in which both can be compared.

DG speaks only in grammar terms: ground, distinction, relation, constraint, capability, inheritance, collapse, translation, closure, convergence.

## 7\. Instantiation Sketches

A framework instantiates DG only if it supplies: object mapping, relation mapping, constraint mapping, root layer, capability sequence, dependency graph, collapse conditions, translation rules, closure checks, grade labels, and kill conditions.

THEOPHYSICS INSTANTIATION

**Ground:** God as necessary source

**Distinction:** Creation/Creator; identity and difference

**Relation:** Logos-mediated order; lawful relation

**Constraint:** Truth / orientation / goodness

**Capability:** Existence, distinction, relation, orientation, information, witness, agency

**Collapse:** Loss of coherence, contradiction, moral rupture

HEBUN-ZANABUN INSTANTIATION

**Ground:** Ehad / undivided source

**Distinction:** Named emanations or structured differentiation

**Relation:** Ordered dependence among revealed modes

**Constraint:** Unity-preserving lawfulness

**Capability:** Source, expression, witness, obedience, return

**Collapse:** Fragmentation, loss of unity, false autonomy

The point is not to declare the traditions identical. The point is to test whether their derivation structures preserve equivalent dependency, capability, collapse, and translation behavior.

## 8\. Independent Convergence Grades

Grade| Meaning
---|---
IC-0| No meaningful convergence
IC-1| Vocabulary resemblance only
IC-2| Shared sequence but weak dependency match
IC-3| Shared dependency graph
IC-4| Shared dependency graph plus collapse behavior
IC-5| Shared dependency graph, capability classes, collapse behavior, and translation behavior

Only IC-4 or IC-5 can support a serious convergence claim. Anything below is analogy, resonance, or suggestive pattern.

HONEST BOUNDARY

This document does not prove God, Christ, Trinity, Ehad, Islam, Christianity, Theophysics, Hebun-Zanabun, the Master Equation, consciousness, or morality. It does not rank worldviews.

It provides a grammar for comparison. It tests whether claimed derivations are admissible, whether dependencies are preserved, whether translations are honest, and whether independent frameworks share more than vocabulary.

The Lean kernel is not yet written for v0.2. Independent Convergence is not yet proved. It is the most important proposed formal criterion introduced by v0.2.

DG does not tell a worldview what to name the ground.
It tests whether the worldview can keep the ground, the layers,
and the consequences in the right order.

The grammar is first. The instantiations come after.
