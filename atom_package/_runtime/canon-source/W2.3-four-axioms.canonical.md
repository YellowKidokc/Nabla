# The Four Axioms, Taken Cold

No God. No Scripture. No interpretation.

Four assumptions, stated plainly — and everything that follows from them.

David Lowe · Sheet 00A · A-Track Charter

Part I — The Rules of This Track

## What this series is, _and what it refuses to be_

This is the parallel spine. Every sheet numbered with an A runs on axioms, mathematics, and physics alone. The discipline is total and checkable:

  1. No theological vocabulary. Not as metaphor, not as color, not once. If a word needs a church to define it, it does not appear in an A-sheet.

  2. Every citation is to philosophy, mathematics, or physics. Nothing else is load-bearing.

  3. Every conclusion must survive a reader who rejects every religious claim ever made. That reader is the intended audience.

  4. Any smuggled premise found in an A-sheet is a defect. Report it; it gets cut in the open.


If you believe nothing beyond what can be argued, this track is yours. Take it on. See if you can beat it.

Part II — The Axioms

## Four assumptions. _Stated, not hidden._

Every formal system pays its way in axioms. ZFC pays nine. Peano pays five. General relativity pays two postulates; quantum mechanics pays five. Nobody calls those systems theology. This track pays four — and unlike most systems, it itemizes the bill on page one.

A1 — Existence

Something exists.

The one assumption no argument can precede, since arguments exist. Why there is something rather than nothing — "for nothing is simpler and easier than something" Leibniz, Principles of Nature and Grace §7, 1714 — is a question this track does not answer. It takes the something as given.

A2 — Distinction

Difference is real.

This is not that; a thing is not its negation. Aristotle, Metaphysics IV From real difference, information is possible: a distinction that can be registered is a bit. Shannon, A Mathematical Theory of Communication, 1948 Without A2 there is no measurement, no thought, no this sentence.

A3 — Relation

Distinguishable things interact lawfully.

Regularly, repeatably, everywhere tested. Induction cannot ground itself — no experiment proves that experiments generalize Hume, Enquiry IV, 1748 — so lawfulness is taken as an axiom, exactly as every laboratory on Earth silently takes it.

A4 — Orientation

Some states rank above others.

On at least one real gradient: signal above noise, truth above error, coherence above dissolution. No "ought" follows from any pile of "is" Hume, Treatise III.i.1, 1739 — so the gradient cannot be derived and is posited instead. Note what denying it costs: the denial "this axiom is false" claims truth beats error, which is the gradient in use. A4 may be held as objective value or as the minimal ranking any inquiry presupposes; the track runs on either reading.

Four axioms. Itemized, priced, paid in the open. Everything after this line is derivation, and every derivation can be checked.

Part III — What This Track Does Not Do

## The debts stay unpaid — _on purpose_

The companion series asks why these four cohere and offers an answer. This track does not ask. Here the four are brute: unexplained, unapologized for, exactly as brute as spacetime's postulates or set theory's nine. The refusal is a feature. It means every result downstream belongs to anyone who accepts the four — which, as the axiom statements show, is anyone who has ever measured, argued, or called a claim false.

Part IV — Where the Track Goes

## The road, _axioms only_

01A

**The single expression:** space, matter, motion, time admit no ordering — each is constituted through the others. Relational space against the absolute container Leibniz–Clarke Correspondence, 1715–16; geometry and content co-defining Einstein 1915; Wheeler; time as the number of motion Aristotle, Physics IV.11. One package, no first member.

02A

**The grammar:** any system maintaining structure against noise needs an ordering rate, an information density, and a possibility space — a coherence functional as schema, with its open formalization problems stated, not hidden.

03A

**The cost:** information is physical; ordering has a price Landauer 1961; Shannon 1948. Structure far from equilibrium persists only by exporting entropy Prigogine, dissipative structures, Nobel 1977. The tension that builds also dissolves — and that tension is where complexity lives.

04A

**The receiver:** systems that register distinctions and act on gradients — observation, agency, and the ranking A4 already priced in.

The single expression in 01A resolves into six concrete instantiations, none of which can be ordered before the others:

  * **Information** — as distinguished content
  * **Order** — as directed relation
  * **Space** — as domain of co-existent relations
  * **Time** — as sequential relation
  * **Matter** — as instantiated physical content
  * **Motion** — as ordered change


Part V — The Challenge

## Beat it, _or absorb it_

The claim of this track is narrow and testable: from these four axioms, the chain that follows is valid — each step forced, no step smuggled. To defeat it, do one of two things: show a fifth hidden axiom doing secret work, or break a derivation step. Either kill is accepted publicly and the sheet gets amended in the open.

What would kill it

A fifth hidden axiom found doing secret work anywhere in the chain, or one broken derivation step under the same definitions. One verified case, publicly reported, and the sheet is amended in the open. That invitation stands permanently.

And if the chain holds? Then its conclusions stand on _your_ floor, whoever you are — because you already paid these four axioms the moment you started checking. What remains, for anyone who wants it, is a further question this track is forbidden to ask: four brute assumptions, or one ground that pays for all four and explains why they cohere. A companion series takes that question up. This one never needs it.

Four axioms. One chain.
Break it if you can.
