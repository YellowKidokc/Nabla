## Newton–Grace

Gravitation · Sin and Grace

### The Physical Law, Written Out

$$F = \dfrac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2}$$

**F** the pull between two bodies · **G** = 6.674×10⁻¹¹ m³kg⁻¹s⁻² · **m₁, m₂** the masses · **r** the separation.

_Every piece of matter pulls on every other piece. Harder if either is heavier, weaker as the square of the distance — double the distance, quarter the pull._

And the deeper form, which is the one that governs:

$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \dfrac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}$$

_Matter tells spacetime how to curve. Curved spacetime tells matter how to move. Gravity is not a force — it is the shape of the space._

### Six Things About Gravity That Are Strange

**It is the weakest force by an absurd margin** — about 10³⁶ times weaker than electromagnetism. A coin-sized magnet lifts a paperclip against the pull of six trillion trillion kilograms of Earth.

**And it runs the universe anyway, because it never cancels.** Charge comes in two signs and bulk matter is neutral. The nuclear forces reach 10⁻¹⁵ m and stop. Gravity has one sign, infinite range, no negative mass. Every gram adds. Nothing subtracts. The weakest thing there is, given time and accumulation, decides the shape of everything.

**No shielding exists.** A metal box blocks an electric field — that is a Faraday cage, and your microwave door is one. There is no gravitational equivalent. No material, no geometry, no configuration.

**Everything falls identically.** Inertial and gravitational mass agree to fifteen decimal places. Feather and hammer, dropped on the Moon in 1971, landed together on camera. Gravity does not care what you are made of.

**It does not settle.** Remove energy from a normal system and it cools. Remove energy from a self-gravitating system and the core _contracts and heats_. Negative specific heat. There is no stable resting point to relax into — pull a little and it pulls harder. The endpoint has a name: the gravothermal catastrophe.

**Past a threshold,_out_ stops being a direction.**

$$r_s = \dfrac{2GM}{c^2}$$

Inside rs, escape is not difficult. Escape is not a direction that exists. Every future-directed path leads inward. The singularity is in your future the way tomorrow is.

### The Spiritual Law — Collapse

Read that list again.

Weakest thing there is. Never cancels. Always accumulates. Cannot be shielded. Falls on everyone identically regardless of what they are made of. Does not settle — pulls harder the more it pulls. Past a threshold, _out_ is not a direction.

That is sin, stage by stage, and nobody arranged it.

Not sin as a force pushing you. **Sin as accumulated curvature.** The weakest possible influence — one small wrong thing, so slight it barely registers — that cancels against nothing, and so accumulates, and the accumulation bends the space you move through, and the bent space draws in more, and that deepens the bend. Augustine knew the geometry before anyone had the equation: _incurvatus in se_ — the soul curved in on itself.

And there is a point past which people describe the horizon exactly. Not _I can't get out._ **I can't see out. I don't know what out would even look like from here.** That is not despair as a feeling. It is a geometry.

### The Spiritual Law — Grace

Grace is not a second force. Same field, same equation, different regime. **Grace is the orbit.**

Add transverse motion and the effective potential becomes:

$$V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r) = -\dfrac{GM\mu}{r} + \dfrac{L^2}{2\mu r^2}$$

where **L** is angular momentum — the component of motion that is not heading straight in, and **μ** is the reduced mass of the orbiting pair. Set the derivative to zero:

$$r_* = \dfrac{L^2}{GM\mu} \quad \text{exists if and only if } L \neq 0$$
$$L = 0 \implies \text{monotone infall — no stationary point anywhere}$$

**Two regimes, one equation, and the switch is L.**

And then Einstein, which is the whole thing: **a body in orbit is in free fall the entire time. It feels no force at all.** An accelerometer on a satellite reads zero. The astronauts are weightless because they are _falling and missing_. The orbiting body is not pulled around a corner. It is going straight. The space is curved. Held, no force applied, traveling the straightest path available to it the whole way — and the curvature is what makes that straight line come home instead of leave.

### The Interpretation — The Center of the Law

In general relativity, the body falling into the black hole and the planet in its orbit are doing exactly the same thing. Both in free fall. Both on geodesics. Neither feels force. The difference is not in what they do. The difference is the curvature they do it in.

Sin and grace are not opposite actions. They are the same motion — going where the shape of things takes you — in two regimes of one field. The man collapsing and the man carried are both following the path in front of them. One path closes. One comes home. Grace is not a force applied to you. **It is a change in the shape of the space.**

### The Kill Test, Answered — With a Conservation Law

The question was: _what is the theological equivalent of angular momentum that is not self-salvation?_

$$\dfrac{dL}{dt} = \tau_{\mathrm{ext}}$$

**Angular momentum is conserved. A closed system cannot change its own total angular momentum.** No external torque, no change in total L — Noether's theorem from rotational symmetry. If the system initially lacks the orbital motion required to avoid collapse, and its permitted internal rearrangements cannot create it without an equal counter-motion preserving the collapse condition, then escape requires altered boundary or initial conditions.

Grace is the given geometry and sustaining condition that makes a non-collapsing path possible before the soul can save itself. The orbit is received before it is maintained.

That is prevenient grace stated as a conservation constraint — Augustine against Pelagius, closed by mechanics. And notice the company: Newton's third law, the Second Law of thermodynamics, conservation of angular momentum — three conservation laws, three independent constraints on self-rescue.

**"The inverse-square exponent is inherited, not derived."** Largely conceded. The one real constraint: Bertrand's theorem (1873) — among all central-force potentials, exactly two give closed stable orbits. If you require a relationship that _returns_ , the exponent is one of two, not free. Suggestive, not decisive.

**"Real orbits decay."** True — and it helps: the orbit requires sustaining. Law 5 reaches the same requirement by an entirely different road.
